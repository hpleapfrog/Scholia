> **English version of [`电荷-AL-你摸到的一切力都是电磁力.md`](电荷-AL-你摸到的一切力都是电磁力.md).**

## What the method does

The `[AL]` prompt is a **sweeping claim**:

> `[AL] Explain that all the macroscopic forces that we experience directly, such as the sensations of touch and the tension in a rope, are due to the electromagnetic force, one of the four fundamental forces in nature.`

Then gravity — but **by a different route**:

> `The gravitational force… is actually sensed through the electromagnetic interaction of molecules, such as between those in our feet and those on the top of a bathroom scale.`

**Gravity is not electromagnetic; feeling gravity is.** The closing line draws the boundary:

> `The other two fundamental forces, the strong nuclear force and the weak nuclear force, cannot be sensed on the human scale.`

| Not suitable for | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| students who have not met the four interactions | the whole content is classification | **strong** |
| students currently learning charging and conservation | this is a sidebar to the chapter opening, not the main line | **medium** |
| students who need to solve problems today | produces no problem-solving skill | **medium** |

## Risks

| Where it goes wrong | Why |
|---|---|
| saying "gravity is also electromagnetic" | the textbook separates them with "sensed through molecular electromagnetic interaction"; **merging them destroys the point** |
| stopping at "touch is electromagnetic" | the nuclear-force boundary is part of the statement |
| leaving students with "electromagnetism explains everything" | the claim is limited to **macroscopic, directly experienced** forces |
