## What the method does

The source opens with two questions, no conclusions:

> `[BL] [OL] Discuss with students what may cause light to eject electrons from a material. Are there certain materials that are more susceptible to having electrons ejected?`

Mechanism first, then **material** — the second question is where "work function" will land.

Note the naming order in the body: phenomenon → Einstein's quantisation → *then* the word `photons`.
**The name arrives last.**

## Contraindications

| Not suitable for | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| students who have not met electrons | both questions are about electrons | **strong** |
| students who already know `E = hf` and the work function | they need the regularities explained | **medium** |
| a lesson that must compute threshold frequency now | produces no calculation | **medium** |

## Risks

| Where it goes wrong | Why |
|---|---|
| writing `E_k = hf − W` first | the order is guess → phenomena → quantisation |
| skipping "are some materials more susceptible" | that question is the door to the work function |
| saying "brighter light means faster electrons" | wrong: intensity sets the number, frequency sets the maximum kinetic energy |
