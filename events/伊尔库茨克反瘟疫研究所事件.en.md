## Statement

**Event**: a female laboratory worker at the "Irkutsk Anti-Plague Research Institute of Siberia and the
Far East" (in the town of Shelekhov, Irkutsk region, Russia) died in early October 2026. The official
preliminary diagnosis was "**pneumonia of unknown etiology**"; whether she died of **pneumonic plague**
was raised by several parties but **was never confirmed**. The institute's laboratory and the local
hospital were quarantined, and about 200 contacts were placed under medical observation.

| 2026-10-08 | **Russia lifted the quarantine** in Irkutsk and told WHO there was "**no plague case**" | ev-066 |

### Unresolved contradictions (as of the last update)

1. **Cause of death**: Rospotrebnadzor says "not plague"; the Buryatia head says "possibly plague";
   WHO says it is checking whether it was pneumonic plague — **the three accounts conflict**.
2. **Lab leak**: a "broken tube of live bacteria" was reported; Russian officials deny finding a leak.
3. **Second case**: reported by media; denied by Russia.
4. **Transparency**: on 10-07 WHO said explicitly that information was insufficient to fully assess.

> **This entry records the official accounts and their contradictions only; it does not decide whether
