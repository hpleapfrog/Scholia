> it makes no truth judgement and carries no argument. Much speculation surrounds this timeline
> ("EVALI was really COVID-19", "COVID-19 was in the U.S. much earlier", "the virus spread through U.S.
> military blood programmes"); this entry downgrades all of it to **open questions** and states, for every

## Statement

**Event**: in 2019 the United States saw an outbreak of severe lung injury of initially unknown cause,
later named **e-cigarette, or vaping, product use–associated lung injury (EVALI)**. The final official
tally (as of 2020-02-18): **2,807 hospitalisations and 68 deaths** reported nationwide. The mainstream
conclusion was **chemical exposure** (vitamin E acetate and other substances in illicit THC cartridges),
not an infection.

Immediately adjacent in time, at the end of December 2019 Wuhan reported a cluster of "pneumonia of
unknown cause" to WHO, and the COVID-19 pandemic followed — WHO declared a PHEIC on 2020-01-30, countries
moved into states of emergency and lockdowns in March 2020, and on 2023-05-05 WHO declared that COVID-19
**no longer constitutes a PHEIC**. Then, from 2020, **blood-donation serology, wastewater monitoring
and retrospective testing of stored samples** suggested SARS-CoV-2 **may (possibly, and later likely)**
have been present at **low level** in several countries before 2020-01-19 — the date the first U.S. case
was identified.
**How the virus first entered the human population remains undetermined.**

Online discussion often stitches these official strands into claims such as "COVID-19 really started with
the American vaping illness". **This entry makes no such judgement**: it keeps the official timeline, the
official positions and the open questions side by side, so that every claim can be traced to a source.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why record this**: EVALI's confirmed case definition required "negative respiratory viral panel" and
> similar exclusions — and the 2019 panels **could not have included** SARS-CoV-2, which had not yet been
> discovered. That leaves a real **testing gap**. A gap is a blank, not evidence; whether any 2019 EVALI
> case was in fact COVID-19 **cannot be ruled out retrospectively at the testing level**. Whether any
> official systematic retrospective testing was done, and with what result, is listed under "Open
> questions" below.

### 6. Unresolved contradictions and open questions

1. **The testing gap**: the confirmed EVALI case definition required ruling out respiratory infection, but the 2019 panels **could not have included** SARS-CoV-2 testing, which did not exist. Whether any 2019 EVALI case was actually COVID-19 therefore **cannot be ruled out at the testing level**. **As of this repo's update date, this repo has not found any official publication of systematic retrospective SARS-CoV-2 testing of 2019 EVALI cases** (this reflects what our searches found; it is not a proof of absence).
2. **The limits of "possibly earlier"**: both blood-donation studies use the wording *possibly / likely*, and both note that **at low prevalence, false positives and cross-reactivity cannot be fully excluded**. They support **earlier, low-level** presence — **not** widespread community transmission in 2019, and **not** any link to EVALI. The same applies to the retrospective studies from Italy (blood, wastewater, a child's swab), France, Brazil and "All of Us" — their sampling frames, assay characteristics and false-positive risks differ; this repo does not merge evidence grades across studies. Each paper states its own limitations.
3. **Origins unresolved**: the 2021 joint study said the role of the Huanan market **cannot be determined conclusively**; SAGO (2025) points to zoonotic spillover as "the weight of available evidence" while **key data remain unavailable** and the work is "unfinished"; WHO keeps appealing for information sharing. Two Science papers (2022) and one Cell paper (2024) analysing Huanan-market samples support a market-introduction scenario (see section 3).
4. **The infection chains of the early positives are unknown**: for the 2019-12-13–16 antibody-reactive donors, the Italian antibody-positive subjects from 2019-09, the child whose Milan swab was positive on 2019-12-05, the French patient of 2019-12-27 and the local transmission implied by the Brazilian sewage detection of 2019-11-27, no paper or WHO material says where the infections came from or whether related cases exist.
5. **The boundary of "U.S. military facility / blood programme" claims**: from 2021, media articles proposed specific chains such as "the virus spread from Fort Detrick and other bases to Europe via the U.S. Armed Services Blood Program (ASBP)"; **as far as this repo's searches go, no official investigative conclusion or official statement supports these chains**. Nor has this repo found any published retrospective SARS-CoV-2 testing of 2019 U.S. military personnel serum samples. The only official records are the facts in section 4. (This reflects what our searches found; it is not a proof of absence.)
