## Statement

### 1. The condition for confirmation: spirometry plus clinical context

| Provision | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic criterion | In the appropriate clinical context, **non-fully reversible airflow obstruction measured by spirometry — i.e. FEV1/FVC < 0.7 post-bronchodilation — confirms the diagnosis of COPD** | ev-079 |
| Definition of the disease | A **heterogeneous lung condition** characterised by chronic respiratory symptoms (dyspnoea, cough, sputum production and/or exacerbations) due to abnormalities of the **airways (bronchitis, bronchiolitis) and/or alveoli (emphysema)** that cause **persistent, often progressive, airflow obstruction** | ev-079 |
| Goals of initial assessment | Determine the severity of airflow obstruction, assess the impact of current symptoms, and assess the risk of future events (exacerbations, hospital admissions, death) **in order to guide therapy** | ev-079 |

### 2. The substantive 2026 change: the E-group threshold drops to "1"

> **Verbatim from GOLD's official *KEY CHANGES SUMMARY* and the report body:**
>
> > The criteria defining GOLD A, B and E categories have been adjusted due to emerging evidence from observational studies that **even one moderate or severe exacerbation prior to initiating maintenance pharmacological therapy increases the risk of subsequent events**.
> >
> > The threshold of **one moderate exacerbation** should now be used to consider **treatment escalation**, with the aim of achieving a **low disease activity state characterized by no exacerbations**.

| Change | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Grouping criteria | The criteria defining **A, B and E have been adjusted**, on the grounds that observational studies show **even one moderate or severe exacerbation before starting maintenance pharmacotherapy increases the risk of subsequent events** | ev-079 |
| Escalation threshold | **One moderate exacerbation** is now enough to consider **treatment escalation** | ev-079 |
| Treatment goal | Achieve a **low disease activity state characterised by no exacerbations** | ev-079 |
| New section | A new section on **Disease Activity** has been added | ev-079 |
| Structural distinction | Chapter 3 now clarifies the distinction between **INITIAL** pharmacological treatment (patients naïve to maintenance therapy) and **FOLLOW-UP** pharmacological treatment (patients already on treatment) | ev-079 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is the most substantive change of the 2026 edition relative to earlier years.**
> Previously the E group was drawn at "≥2 exacerbations"; the 2026 edition moves the line to **1**,
> meaning **high-risk patients are identified — and escalation considered — earlier**.
> This entry records only that the line moved; **it does not evaluate whether the change is right.**

### 3. Provisions on the acute exacerbation and on initial treatment

| Provision | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| General rule | **Pharmacological therapy should be started as soon as possible** to prevent complications and subsequent events | ev-079 |
| Initial bronchodilators | For moderate/severe exacerbations: **SABAs, with or without short-acting anticholinergics** | ev-079 |
| Systemic corticosteroids | For moderate/severe exacerbations: **up to 5 days** | ev-079 |
| Antibiotics | **A total of 5 days** (for purulent sputum, prior history of lung infections, etc.) | ev-079 |
| Methylxanthines | **Not recommended**, due to increased side-effect profiles | ev-079 |
| Maintenance treatment | **Maintenance therapy with LABDs should be initiated as soon as possible** | ev-079 |
| Acute respiratory failure | High-flow oxygen and mechanical NIV are indicated: they improve gas exchange, reduce work of breathing and the need for intubation, shorten hospital stay and improve survival | ev-079 |
| How initial treatment is individualised | Guided by **severity of symptoms, risk of exacerbations, side-effects, comorbidities, drug availability and cost, and the patient's preference and ability to use various drug delivery devices** | ev-079 |
| Lung-cancer screening | **Annual LDCT** for patients with a smoking history, similar to the recommendation for the general population | ev-079 |

## Arguments

### A1 — Everything in `Statement` is verbatim GOLD 2026 text (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: Sections 1–3 are provisions **written down explicitly** in the GOLD 2026 report /
  pocket guide / key-changes summary, not this repo's inference or paraphrase
- **Premise**: The main evidence is the three official GOLD documents (ev-079)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. For claims of the form "what does a guideline prescribe", **the guideline text itself is the
     primary source** — same as `ev-049` (AASM), `ev-071`–`ev-073` (plague) and `ev-078` (Chinese
     hypertension guideline) in this repo
  2. The two sentences in section 2 are **quoted word for word in English**; verification means
     returning to the original, not trusting this repo's rendering
  3. The numbers in section 3 (5 days, 5 days, once a year, FEV1/FVC < 0.7) are all
     **specific provisions locatable in the original**
- **Evidence**: ev-079
- **Attacked by**: **this repo read key passages of three official documents and did not read the
  report in full**; moreover the **full ABE grouping also has a symptom side (mMRC / CAAT cut-offs)**,
  which this repo did not read — so section 2 states only the **exacerbation side** of the line, and a
  reader may mistake that for the complete ABE grouping
- **Response**: **The scope of this entry is fixed by what this repo actually read in the original**;
  the incompleteness is declared explicitly in disclaimer point 4, and the complete grouping rule is
  listed under `To be added`
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-16

### A2 — "GOLD prescribes it" is neither "this repo recommends it" nor "it is best" (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: This entry only claims **GOLD 2026 says so**; it does not claim these provisions have
  been validated to any particular degree of effectiveness, nor does it constitute any recommendation
- **Premise**: The discipline's three-layer boundary (see the discipline README, "one revision of the
  boundary"): layer 2, "operating parameters stated verbatim in a guideline", **may be included**,
  provided with a verifiable locator, population limits and an explicit disclaimer; layer 3,
  "decisions about an individual patient", **may not**
- **Reasoning**:
  1. The three kinds of content here (diagnostic criterion, revision of the grouping criteria,
     exacerbation-stage medication durations) are all **verbatim provisions** — layer 2
  2. "Does this person have COPD, which drug, for how many days" is layer 3 — **none of it is written
     here**
  3. The GOLD report is **revised every year** (the 2026 edition is itself a "major revision");
     this entry records the release dates of all three documents precisely so readers can check
     currency themselves
- **Evidence**: ev-079
- **Attacked by**: **the textbook evidence (ev-074 · ev-075) has never been opened by this repo**,
  yet it is listed under `evidence:` — a reader may mistake that for textbook-level cross-verification
- **Response**: **This is a known weak point of this entry, stated twice** — under `Evidence` and in
  section 4 of `Statement`: the two textbooks have `核验深度` of `仅定位` and **serve only as
  background, not as support**; `status` stays at `暂定确认` rather than `已确证` precisely because
  the independent-review condition is not met
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-16

  the symptom-side ABE cut-offs (mMRC / CAAT)**; the original text of the preferred first-line
