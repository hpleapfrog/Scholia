> [!CAUTION]
> **Read this block first. This is not medical advice; it is a record of what three official guidelines say.**
>
> 1. **Not for any individual patient.** Recognising and managing plague requires clinical judgement
>    from qualified personnel — this repo cannot do that and does not take on that task.
>    **Anyone with relevant symptoms should seek medical care immediately, not act on this text.**
> 2. **The guidelines delimit their own scope.** The WHO manual addresses the public-health level of
>    **surveillance, diagnosis, prevention and control**; the CDC guidance covers **both naturally
>    acquired infections and bioterrorism events** (ev-071 · ev-072 · ev-073).
> 3. **This entry records what the guidelines prescribe, not what you should do.** Every item cites
>    its source; **no steps, no prescriptions, no judgement about whether a given person is a suspected case.**
> 4. **This entry contains no surveillance or notification (IHR) provisions** — that belongs to
>    public-health governance, outside this discipline's boundary (Medicine · Clinical Guidelines);
>    none of it is included here.
> 5. **This repo only states what these guidelines say**; it does not extrapolate for them, nor
>    judge whether they are still the current versions.

## Statement

### 1. The most counterintuitive rule: treat before confirmation

| Source | What the guideline states |
|---|---|
| **WHO manual** (ev-071) | During an outbreak, for patients with clinical suspicion of any form of plague, **treatment is started independently of F1 RDT results and does not rely on a positive test** — verbatim: `treatment should be started independently of F1RDT findings and does not rely on a positive test` |
| **CDC** (ev-072) | **"DO NOT WAIT FOR DIAGNOSTIC TEST RESULTS IF YOU SUSPECT PLAGUE"**; **"Never delay or withhold treatment pending the receipt of laboratory test results"** — verbatim |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This runs against the common intuition: confirm first, then medicate.**
> Plague — pneumonic plague especially — progresses fast, so **the waiting time itself is a risk**.
> The guidelines' position is: **treat on suspicion, adjust once results are in.**
> "Do not wait for confirmation" does not mean "do not diagnose" — tests are still run,
> but **treatment is not made conditional on them.**

### 2. Treatment timing and duration

| Rule | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Antimicrobials within **24 hours** of symptom onset, to reduce the chance of death | ev-072 |
| Duration | **10–14 days** | ev-072 |
| FDA-approved antimicrobials | Streptomycin, ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin, moxifloxacin, doxycycline | ev-073 |
| Update to first-line choices | The WHO's 2021 guidelines list **fluoroquinolones** among first-line options | ev-071 |

### 3. Isolation

| Rule | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Suspected, probable or confirmed **pneumonic** plague patients | ev-072 |
| How | **Standard + droplet precautions** | ev-072 |
| How long | For at least the **first 48 hours** of antimicrobial therapy, until clinical improvement or until plague is ruled out | ev-072 |
| Mass-casualty situations | Patients may be **cohort-managed**; droplet precautions may stop after 48 hours of therapy, standard precautions continue | ev-072 |

### 4. Prophylaxis for contacts

| Rule | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Post-exposure prophylaxis | Close contacts requiring PEP receive **7 days** of antimicrobials | ev-072 |

> [!NOTE]
> **Do not conflate two things**: this entry records the **guidelines' written provisions**;
> "is this patient plague, which drug, what dose" is a **clinical decision** —
> **none of it is written here**, and none of it should be inferred from these numbers.

## Arguments

### A1 · Every line in the tables above is verbatim guideline text (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: Each item in sections 1–4 is a provision **written down explicitly** in a WHO/CDC
  guideline, not this repo's inference or paraphrase
- **Premise**: All three sources are guidelines/recommendation reports publicly issued by official
  agencies (ev-071 · ev-072 · ev-073)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. For claims of the form "what does a guideline prescribe", **the guideline text itself is the
     primary source** — same as this repo's `ev-049` (AASM guideline); no place on the evidence ladder
     is needed
  2. "Treat without waiting for confirmation" appears in **two independent documents, WHO and CDC** —
     **not the claim of a single source**
  3. The timing provisions (24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days, 10–14 days) are **specific numbers locatable
     in the original**; verification means going back to the text, not trusting this repo's rendering
- **Evidence**: ev-071 · ev-072 · ev-073
- **Attacked by**: **this repo read only key passages of all three documents, never the full text**
  (see the verification note under `Evidence`) — the table's contents are traceable, but **this repo's
  completeness check has not been exhaustive**
- **Response**: **This entry only states what those passages say**; claiming "all provisions of these
  guidelines" would require a full-text check (to be added)
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-14

### A2 · "The guideline prescribes it" is neither "this repo recommends it" nor "it is best" (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: This entry only claims **the guidelines say so**; it does not claim these provisions have
  been validated to any particular degree of effectiveness, nor does it constitute any recommendation
- **Premise**: This discipline's boundary (see the discipline README, "one revision of the boundary"),
  three layers: layer 2, "operating parameters stated verbatim in a guideline", **may be included**,
  provided with a verifiable locator, population limits and an explicit disclaimer; layer 3, "decisions
  about an individual patient", **may not**
- **Reasoning**:
  1. The four kinds of content here (treatment timing, duration, isolation window, contact prophylaxis
     duration) are all **verbatim provisions** — layer 2
  2. "Is this patient suspected, which drug, what dose" is layer 3 — **none of it is written here**
  3. Guideline provisions **change as evidence and the epidemiological situation change**; this entry
     records source and date precisely so readers can check currency themselves
- **Evidence**: ev-071 · ev-072 · ev-073
- **Attacked by**: **the full answer to "what is needed when plague returns" also includes surveillance,
  notification, strategic stockpiles, vaccination and other public-health components** — this entry covers
  only clinical management and contact management, and a reader may mistake that for the whole answer
- **Response**: **The scope of this entry is fixed by the discipline boundary** (Medicine · Clinical
  Guidelines). Surveillance and notification (IHR) belong to public-health governance; vaccination
  provisions belong to population-level prevention — if they are to be included, they belong to another
  discipline or a separate entry; disclaimer point 4 states explicitly that they are excluded here
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-14
