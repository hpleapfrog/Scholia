# Transport

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This subject holds "what the traffic rules are", not "how to get there or how to file".**
>
> It reuses the two criteria this repo has already established: **scarcity** (anything the
> on-site signage is obliged to provide, the repo does not repeat) and **risk** (procedures
> that can hurt you are not held — see
> [decision record: the boundary of procedural knowledge](../../docs/decisions/procedural-knowledge-boundary.en.md)).
> So what goes here are **obligations, numbers and prohibitions in laws and standards** —
> **"who must do what", "what is forbidden"** — each one checkable against the original clause.

## Boundary

| | Holds | Does not hold |
|---|---|---|
| content | obligations, numbers and prohibitions in laws, administrative regulations, ministerial rules and national standards that are **checkable item by item** | filing procedures, operational steps, travel routes |
| source | officially published, with a document number or effective date | unsourced paraphrase, second-hand summaries |
| phrasing | "document X stipulates Y (doc no. · clause no. · to be verified)" | "you should do Y" |

**Clause numbers are always marked as to-be-verified**: this repo does not write clause numbers
from memory. A locator that has not been read back against the original text is left unfilled.
