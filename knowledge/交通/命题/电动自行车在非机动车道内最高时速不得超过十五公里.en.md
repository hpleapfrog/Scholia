## Statement

Article 58 of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the PRC provides:

> 残疾人机动轮椅车、电动自行车在非机动车道内行驶时，最高时速不得超过十五公里。(Art. 58)

So **fifteen kilometres per hour** is the statutory ceiling for these two classes of vehicle **while driving in a non-motor-vehicle lane**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This number is easy to misremember, so this entry claims only what the text says.**
>
> This entry claims **only** the road-passage speed limit for "**driving in a non-motor-vehicle lane**".
> It **does not touch**, and does not stand for, the vehicle's design speed at manufacture, any
> production standard, or the limit in any other situation — those are other texts, **not yet
> registered here**, and so **outside the statement of this entry**.

## Arguments

### A1 · The text itself gives the number (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: fifteen kilometres per hour is **the statute's explicit text**, not industry practice and not enforcement practice
- **Premise**: Article 58 writes the vehicle class (motorised wheelchairs, electric bicycles), the road situation (driving in a non-motor-vehicle lane) and the ceiling (fifteen) into one article (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. This is "**what is prohibited / what the number is**" — exactly what the [traffic](../README.en.md) subject collects
  2. It is **falsifiable by the reader**: open the official page and search "第五十八条"
  3. The qualifier and the number sit in the same sentence, so **the qualifier cannot be stripped** —
     drop "in a non-motor-vehicle lane" and the number changes context
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: see A2
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12

### A2 · This entry claims one version only, and one layer only (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this entry claims "**the version amended on 2021-04-29 says so**", **not "it still holds now"**; and it claims only the **road-passage speed limit** layer
- **Premise**: the official page carries **no revision number**, only a publication date and the three-amendment history (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. the law **has already been amended three times** — this class of text **does get replaced**
  2. this repo has **no mechanism** to detect a further amendment
  3. and "design speed" and "passage speed limit" are **two different numeric systems**,
     **neither of which this repo has checked** — writing them in would be **beyond the evidence**
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: **this entry has not checked the national standard for electric bicycles, nor any local rule.**
  "Fifteen" is the ceiling for **road passage**; **how fast the vehicle itself can go, and whether its
  limiter can be removed, this entry says not one word about.**
  If a reader reads it as "this vehicle can only be ridden to 15", they have **read past this entry's
  scope** — this entry has not misspoken. That limit is carried in `scope.条件` and the IMPORTANT block above.
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12
- **To fill in**: **the national standard text for electric bicycles** (not yet registered here).
  With it, this proposition can place "passage speed limit" and "design speed" side by side.

## Why this counts as real information rather than noise

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No sign at the edge of a non-motor-vehicle lane reads "limit 15".**
>
> What you can see on site is **the lane itself** (a painted non-motor-vehicle lane);
> what the law writes down is the **numeric ceiling on that lane**.
>
> **This repo's mission is to strip noise and hold true information.**
> That contrast is the test: **what is visible on site, this repo need not repeat;
> what is invisible on site yet written in law is what belongs here.**
> "How fast may I ride my e-bike on this lane" —
> the question has no answer on any sign, and the law gives a definite number.
