## Statement

Article 47 of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the PRC sets **two duties of different strength** for a motor vehicle passing a crosswalk:

| Situation | Duty | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **passing a crosswalk** | must **slow down** | slow down |
| **encountering a pedestrian already crossing** | must **stop and give way** | stop |

The same condition also covers the un-signalled case:

> 机动车行经没有交通信号的道路时，遇行人横过道路，**应当避让**。(Art. 47, para. 2)

Original text (short quote):

> 机动车行经人行横道时，应当减速行驶；遇行人正在通过人行横道，应当停车让行。(Art. 47, para. 1)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The two duties have different triggers and different strength; they are not interchangeable.**
>
> "**Passing** a crosswalk" triggers **slowing down** — no pedestrian need be present;
> "**encountering a pedestrian already crossing**" triggers **stopping to give way** — the pedestrian is already on the crossing.
>
> **"Slow down" is not "give way", and the statute's wording for giving way is "stop and give way".**
> That distinction appears on no sign anywhere.

## Arguments

### A1 · The text itself writes down two layered duties (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: "slow down" and "stop and give way" are **two statutory duties with different triggers**, not two phrasings of one thing
- **Premise**: Article 47 para. 1 puts the two situations side by side across a semicolon; para. 2 gives the un-signalled case separately (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. This is "**who must do what**" — exactly what the [traffic](../README.en.md) subject collects
  2. It is **falsifiable by the reader**: open the official page and search "第四十七条"
  3. the semicolon is itself structure: **the first half's trigger is "passing", the second half's is "encountering a pedestrian already crossing"** — the two do not overlap
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: see A2
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12

### A2 · This entry claims one version only, and touches no fact-finding standard (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this entry claims "**the version amended on 2021-04-29 says so**", **not "it still holds now"**
- **Premise**: the official page carries **no revision number**, only a publication date and the three-amendment history (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. the law **has already been amended three times** — this class of text **does get replaced**
  2. and this repo has **no mechanism** to detect a further amendment
  3. writing "still holds now" into the statement would be **beyond the evidence**
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: **this entry has checked no fact-finding standard, local provision or enforcement approach.**
  "**How slow is slow enough**", "**what counts as 'already crossing'**", "**how 'stop and give way' differs in practice from 'give way'**" —
  **these are fact-finding and discretion, and this repo has looked at none of them.**
  So this entry can only support "**which two duties the statute writes, and what each one's trigger is**",
  **not "what conduct will be found to violate it"**.
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12

## Why this counts as real information rather than noise

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **A sign paints a crosswalk, but does not write out that "slow down" and "stop and give way" are two different duties.**
>
> What you can see on site is **a zebra crossing and possibly a pedestrian**;
> what the law writes down is **two triggers and two strengths**, plus
> **a duty to give way that exists even where there is no traffic signal**.
>
> **This repo's mission is to strip noise and hold true information.**
> That contrast is the test: **what is visible on site, this repo need not repeat;
> what is invisible on site yet written in law is what belongs here.**
> "Is the duty the same with and without a pedestrian" — the question has no answer on any sign,
> while the statute writes them as two sentences.
