## Statement

Article 108 of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the PRC sets out how a fine is paid and what the receipt must be:

> 当事人应当自收到罚款的行政处罚决定书之日起十五日内，到指定的银行缴纳罚款。
> 对行人、乘车人和非机动车驾驶人的罚款，当事人无异议的，可以当场予以收缴罚款。
> 罚款应当开具省、自治区、直辖市财政部门统一制发的罚款收据；
> 不出具财政部门统一制发的罚款收据的，当事人有权拒绝缴纳罚款。(Art. 108)

Taken apart, three things:

| Item | What the law says |
|---|---|
| **Period and place of payment** | within **fifteen days** of receiving the written penalty decision, at the **designated bank** |
| **Exception: collection on the spot** | for fines on **pedestrians, passengers and non-motor-vehicle drivers**, if the party **raises no objection**, it may be collected on the spot |
| **The receipt** | a receipt **uniformly issued by the finance department of the province-level people's government** must be given; **if it is not given, the party may refuse to pay** |

And the penalties that may be decided on the spot have a ceiling of their own:

> 对道路交通违法行为人予以警告、**二百元以下罚款**，交通警察可以当场作出行政处罚决定，并出具行政处罚决定书。(Art. 107)

## Arguments

### A1 · The text itself grants this right (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: "may refuse to pay" is not a rumour, it is **a right granted in the statute's explicit text**, and it carries an explicit condition
- **Premise**: Article 108 paragraph 3 ties the right to refuse directly to a checkable condition —
  **whether a receipt uniformly issued by the provincial-level finance department was given** (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. This is "**who must do what / to whom the legal effect attaches**" — exactly what the [traffic](../README.en.md) subject collects
  2. It is **falsifiable by the reader**: open the official page and search "第一百零八条"
  3. The period (fifteen days), the place (designated bank) and the ceiling (under two hundred yuan may be decided on the spot) are equally explicit, and together with the receipt clause form the complete enforcement structure
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: see A2
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12

### A2 · This entry claims one version only, and is not legal advice (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this entry claims "**the version amended on 2021-04-29 says so**", **not "it still holds now"**
- **Premise**: the official page carries **no revision number**, only a publication date and the three-amendment history (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. the law **has already been amended three times** — this class of text **does get replaced**
  2. and this repo has **no mechanism** to detect a further amendment
  3. writing "still holds now" into the statement would be **beyond the evidence**
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: **this entry has not checked any lower-level rule or local provision.**
  "Uniformly issued by the provincial-level finance department" — **which department that is in a given
  province, what the receipt looks like, how to verify one, this repo has not looked at any of it.**
  So this entry can only support "**the law requires a provincial uniform receipt and ties the right of refusal to that**",
  **not "how you tell whether the slip in your hand is genuine"**.
  The latter needs another text, not yet registered here.
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12
- **To fill in**: **the specimen or the administrative measures for uniform fine receipts of any one provincial finance department**.
  With it, this proposition can reach from "what the law requires" to "how to check it".

## Why this counts as real information rather than noise

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The requirement for a fine receipt hangs on no traffic sign anywhere.**
>
> What you can see on site is **the penalty itself** (a written decision, an amount);
> what the law writes down is **how this money is to be collected, what must be handed over, and what follows if it is not**.
>
> **This repo's mission is to strip noise and hold true information.**
> That contrast is the test: **what is visible on site, this repo need not repeat;
> what is invisible on site yet written in law is what belongs here.**
> "Must this receipt be issued at all" — the question has no answer on any sign.
