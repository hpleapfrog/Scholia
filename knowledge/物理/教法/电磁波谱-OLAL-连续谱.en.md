## Method

**Take apart the impression that "visible light is special", then explain the division of labor between bands with one unifying quantity (penetrating power).**

### 1 · Visible light and invisible radiation differ in no essential way (OL)

Textbook original (short quotation):

> `Dispel any misconceptions that visible light is somehow different from radiation we cannot see,
> except for frequency and wavelength.`

### 2 · The key difference between EM waves and other waves: they can cross a vacuum (OL)

> `Explain that an important difference between EM waves and other waves is that they can travel
> across empty space.`

### 3 · Penetrating power explains what each band is used for (OL / AL)

The textbook gives a main thread that **can explain the whole spectrum**: **penetrating power**.

| Phenomenon | The textbook's explanation |
|---|---|
| the lead apron when having a dental X-ray | X-rays sit at the **high-energy end** and penetrate extremely strongly; **only high-density material (such as lead) can block them** |
| **the greenhouse effect** | the atmosphere is more transparent to **visible light** than to **heat waves**: visible light passes through and heats the surface, while the heat waves the surface radiates are **partly trapped** |
| low-frequency / high-frequency uses differ | it is connected to **penetrating power**, and penetrating power is in turn connected to **health risk** |

### 4 · The AL tier's supplementary classifications (two `[AL]` items)

- electric, magnetic and gravitational fields **are all vector fields**
- the difference between longitudinal and transverse waves; **waves carry energy, not matter**

The textbook has one more `[OL]` item clarifying: **ionizing vs non-ionizing radiation**,
and **electromagnetic radiation vs other radiation such as α/β** — these are two different pairs of
classifications, easily confused.

## Conditions of use

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Level tier | **OL / AL** (textbook markers `[OL]` and `[AL]`) |
| Prerequisites | knows frequency and wavelength; knows waves come as longitudinal or transverse (AL) |
| Cognitive load | medium to high — the load is in **threading the whole spectrum together with one quantity** |

## Contraindications

**Who or what situations does it not apply to?**

| Who or situation it does not apply to | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| students who have not yet formed the concept of wavelength / frequency | breaking up "visible light is special" must land on "it differs only in frequency and wavelength"; without that premise there is nothing to break up | **medium** — inferred from the practice |
| students who have not yet met the concept of a vector (the AL part) | "a field is a vector" requires a basis in vectors | **medium** — inferred from the practice |
| **which tiers** | that `[OL]` item and that `[AL]` item **are two separate passages** — **the textbook gives different content per tier** | —— (this is the marker's **span**, not a contraindication) |
| students **outside this tier** (OL / AL) | the textbook **marks exactly these two tiers for this suggestion**; the BL tier gets a different suggestion | **medium** — the marker explicitly delimits the range, but "not marked" still does not mean "does not apply" — only **untested** |

## Risks

| Where it can go wrong | Why |
|---|---|
| "'no essential difference' gets taught as 'everything is the same'" | the differences are **real** (frequency, wavelength, penetrating power, whether it ionizes); they are just **not a difference in kind at the essential level**. Loose wording turns it into relativism |
| **the two pairs of classifications get blended together** | the textbook specifically clarifies: **ionizing / non-ionizing** is one pair, **EM / αβ** is the other. Mixing them up makes students think "radiation" is one single thing |
| using penetrating power to explain everything | penetrating power explains **the division of labor in uses**, not **why high frequencies carry high energy**; the latter needs photon energy, which is not in this section |
| the greenhouse effect turns into climate politics | the textbook lands on **the difference in penetrating power between frequencies** — an optical fact |

## Argument

### M1 · Designated by the textbook for the OL / AL tiers (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this is the practice the textbook designates for at-grade and advanced students
- **Premise**: the Teacher Support of m54342 marks each of the items above `[OL]` / `[AL]` (ev-030)
- **Reasoning**: the tier marker is the textbook's designation for that level
- **Evidence**: ev-030
- **Attacked**: **"textbook-designated" ≠ "measured to be effective"**; and this practice carries a lot of content, so covering it all in one go may crowd out class time
