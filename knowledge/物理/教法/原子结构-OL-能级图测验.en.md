## Method

**No questions, no discussion — have the students draw with their own hands, and the teacher watches.**

Textbook original (short quotation):

> `Quiz the students at this time. Show a large energy-level diagram on the board.
> Draw an arrow upward and ask whether energy is being emitted or absorbed.
> Have students draw an arrow showing more energy being absorbed.
> Draw a downward arrow and ask whether energy is being emitted or absorbed.
> Have the students compare the amount of energy emitted for various energy-level drops.`

Four consecutive moves:

| # | Teacher does | Students do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | draws an **upward** arrow on the board | judge: absorbed or emitted |
| 2 | — | **draw an arrow themselves showing “more energy being absorbed”** |
| 3 | draws a **downward** arrow | judge: absorbed or emitted |
| 4 | — | **compare the energy emitted for various energy-level drops** |

**Key point**: the textbook uses the word `Quiz` (a quiz), and says `at this time` (**right now**) —
**this is an inserted, on-the-spot check, not an after-class exercise.**

## Conditions of use

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| tier | **OL** (textbook marker `[OL]`) |
| prerequisite | already knows that “energy levels are discrete” and “upward = absorbed / downward = emitted” |
| teaching aid | a **large** energy-level diagram (the textbook stresses `large`) |
| cognitive load | medium — the load sits on **reading direction and magnitude off the diagram** |

## Contraindications

**Who does it not apply to?**

| Who or situation it does not apply to | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| students who have not yet formed the concept “energy levels are discrete” | this method only checks direction and magnitude; if the premise does not stand, all four moves are meaningless | **medium** — inferred from the method |
| students outside this tier | the textbook **gives a different method for each tier** and does not state that this one carries across tiers | **weak** — untested |

## Risks

| Where it can go wrong | Why |
|---|---|
| turning it into a choral answer from the whole class | the textbook's step 2 is “**have the students draw**”. A choral answer only checks a few people; **drawing** is what reveals each individual |
| skipping step 4 | “comparing the energy emitted for various drops” is the **only quantitative step** in this method. Omit it and what remains is only direction-judging |
| the diagram being too small | the textbook specifically writes `large energy-level diagram` — if the diagram is small the back rows cannot see it and the check loses its point |
| “upward = absorbed” memorised as a rule | it comes from energy conservation. Memorise only the direction and a different way of drawing it (energy on the horizontal axis) will throw students off |

## Argument

- **Premise**: the Teacher Support of m54582 marks it with `[OL]` and opens with `Quiz the students at this time` (ev-035)
- **Reasoning**: the tier marker is the textbook's designation for that level
- **Evidence**: ev-035
