## Statement

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This entry is an attack on [proposition: textbooks' built-in teaching variants tiered by learner level](../textbook-tiered-guidance.en.md).**
> It does **not claim** "the textbook gives no level-based suggestions"; it claims only **the entry's count and "note = tier" model do not stand**.

**This is the repo's most far-reaching attack so far** — that proposition has **26 `支持` edges**,
and **26 downstream entries** read `[BL]/[OL]/[AL]` as learner tiers into their `水平档` and `禁忌`.

## Argument

### A1 · "15 support edges" is a drifting number (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: the hard-coded **15** in that proposition does not match measurement
- **Premise**: the original says "**A1 now has 15 `支持` edges**", "these 15 … **each is a manually checked instance**"
- **Reasoning**: measured **26** (during review the attacker observed it grow from 23 to 26, showing it is **still growing**).
  "**now has**" + a drifting number = writing a **snapshot** as an **assertion**.
  And this callout's claim about instance nature ("manually checked") **hangs on the base of 15** — the extra 11 are not covered by it.
- **Incidentally**: `FORMAT.md` §6 actually cited "kbcheck reports 15 supports" as a **normative example** — **the normative document also carried a stale number.**
- **Evidence**: measurement
- **Attacked**: none
- **Strength**: **strong** (reviewed, accepted)

### A2 · The set of "649 tier markers" is mixed with non-tier markers (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: the counter's domain is "four strings", while the claim's domain is "learner **level**" — **the two are not equal**
- **Reasoning**:
  1. `[EL]` = **English Learners** is a **language identity**, not a level tier. It is counted into 649 and **listed alongside the other three as "tier markers"**,
     showing the filter rule is "hits one of the four strings", **not "hits a level tier"**. At most 643 of 649 belong to the "level" dimension.
  2. more fundamentally: **a regular expression carries no meaning.** The grounds can support "the string `[OL]` appeared 245 times",
     but not "[OL] = On Level".
     And these four expansions **appear in the repo only in this one entry** — **no legend of the textbook is cited anywhere.**
     The repo's `(knowledge point × level)` model and the `水平档:` fields of 26 downstream entries **all inherit this one unproven assertion**.
- **Evidence**: ev-020 (this evidence itself has no script or data, see A4)
- **Attacked**: the abbreviations are self-evident (`BL`/`OL`/`AL` are indeed common abbreviations for Below/On/Advanced Level) —
  **I accept this layer**; but `[EL]` is not a matter of self-evident meaning — **it is not the same dimension as "level tier"**
- **Strength**: **medium** (reviewed, accepted)
- **Attribution**: AI subagent proposed · @hpleapfrog reviewed · 2026-10-05

### A3 · "Co-occurrence" cannot yield "attribution" — markers are not tier cards (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: `68 modules containing BL+OL+AL at once` **cannot support** "built-in teaching variants tiered by level"
- **Premise**: the original says "**68 modules contain all three markers BL + OL + AL**", "**split** by marker"
- **Reasoning**:
  1. "tiering / variant" requires **attribution**: which tier a suggestion is for, and the tiers **mutually exclusive**.
     But 649 and 68 provide **occurrence counts and co-occurrence**. **Co-occurrence cannot yield attribution.**
  2. the extraction rule is written as "**split by marker**", which itself presupposes the marker is **a position in linear text** —
     that is the form of **sequence**, not the form of **attribution**.
  3. **measured support (fact 1)**: of 248 notes containing markers, **79% contain 2 or more**;
     `m54108` note 4 has 6 in one note. **All three tiers appearing in one note also fits another reading:
     they are consecutive stages within one note** (do the BL activity first → then ask the OL question → finally pair up and define (AL)).
     The entry's own example **also looks like a sequence**: slide the book (BL) → ask "what is position" (OL) → pair up and define (AL),
     **completable in one class.**
- **Evidence**: ev-020; measurement
- **Attacked**: **markers do label suggestions with target groups** (`[BL][OL]` and `[AL]` point to **different** suggestions),
  so the layer "**labeled by level**" **stands**.
  **What does not stand is the stronger reading my downstream has been using: "the textbook gives a different practice for each tier"** (mutually exclusive alternatives).
- **Strength**: **strong** (reviewed, **partly accepted** — conclusion narrowed as above)
- **Attribution**: AI subagent proposed · @hpleapfrog reviewed · 2026-10-05

### Which layer of `A3` stands and which is weakened

**That stands**: the **co-occurrence count** "68 modules contain BL+OL+AL" indeed cannot support "tiered variants". **The conclusion is unchanged.**

**That is weakened**: this entry suggested markers might be merely **linear sequence markers** ("do this first, then that").
After the script was supplied, the **source file structure** seen is:

```xml
<para><span>[BL]</span><span>[OL]</span> You may want to introduce the concept of a reference point...</para>
<para><span>[AL]</span> Explain that the reference frames considered in this chapter are inertial...</para>
```

**Markers hang on `<para>`**, so they really are **audience labels for a suggestion**, not merely a text position.

**But this entry's other point is reinforced**: `[BL][OL]` **multiple markers** ⇒
**"each tier exclusive" is still wrong**, and the target entry's original framing **still does not stand**.

>
> **`A1′` has not been attacked yet** — **the next adversarial review should attack it directly.**
> **This entry does not change its own status for that**: the conclusions of `A1`–`A5` each still hold.
