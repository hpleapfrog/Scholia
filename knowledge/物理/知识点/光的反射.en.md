> [!TIP]
> **After reading this entry you should** be able to describe reflection with the "normal" and the angle of incidence / angle of reflection, and to distinguish a real focus from a virtual focus

## Definition

The phenomenon of light **returning to its original medium** at an interface is called reflection.

Describing reflection requires three equivalent phrases: **right angle / perpendicular / normal** — all of them refer to the line that makes 90° with the surface.

- **real focus**: light energy actually converges at that point, and can be converted into other forms
- **virtual focus**: **no light actually reaches** that point, so energy cannot converge there
