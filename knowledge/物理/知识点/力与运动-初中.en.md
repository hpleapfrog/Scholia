> **English version of [`力与运动-初中.md`](力与运动-初中.md).**

> [!TIP]
> **After reading this entry you should** be able to judge whether the forces on an object are balanced, and explain that "unbalanced forces change the state of motion"

## Definition

**A force is a push or pull between objects.** The effect of a force is jointly determined by the **object's mass** and the **net force** acting on it.

- **balanced forces** → the object remains at rest or in uniform straight-line motion (stability)
- **unbalanced forces** → the state of motion changes

Kinds of forces include **electric, magnetic, and gravitational** forces; they **can act without contact between objects**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The stage boundary of this knowledge point is explicit**: at the junior-high stage **only conceptual understanding is required**,
> computation is **limited to one-dimensional motion**, and **vectors are not introduced until high school**.
> Textbook wording (short quotation): `Emphasize conceptual understanding … the use of vectors will be introduced in high school.`

## Applicable stage and boundaries

| Dimension | Junior-high requirement | Deferred to high school |
|---|---|---|
| concept of force | conceptual understanding (push/pull, balanced/unbalanced) | vector addition and decomposition |
| computation | **one-dimensional only** | two dimensions, vector operations |
| Newton's laws | first and second laws **qualitatively** | quantitative treatment, formalization of the third law |

> [!NOTE]
> **"What is emphasized and what is deferred" is written here, not left for the teacher to guess.**
> This boundary comes from the textbook's curriculum-standard statement; it is not something I added.
