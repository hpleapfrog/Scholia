## Definition

**A wave** is a way of transferring energy from one place to another —

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **What is transmitted is energy, not matter.**
>
> Water particles only move up and down in place; **they do not travel with the wave**. This is the easiest place to go wrong in this knowledge point,
> see [misconception: waves carry matter along](../误解/waves-carry-matter.en.md).

Three basic classification groups:

| Classification | Basis |
|---|---|
| **mechanical / non-mechanical waves** | whether a medium is required. **Mechanical waves require a medium; electromagnetic waves do not** (they can cross a vacuum) |
| **transverse / longitudinal waves** | the relation between the direction of vibration and the direction of propagation |
| **pulse / periodic waves** | whether it is a single disturbance or a periodic disturbance |

**Basic properties**: **amplitude, period, frequency, wavelength, wave speed**. In the same medium,
**all frequencies of waves travel at the same speed** ⇒ **a change in frequency means a change in wavelength** (`v = f λ`).
