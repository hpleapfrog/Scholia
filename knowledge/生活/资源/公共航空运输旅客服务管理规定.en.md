### Scope (Article 2)

Applies to passenger-service activities of lawfully established **carriers, airport management bodies, ground-service agents, air-sales agents, air-sales network-platform operators, and air-information enterprises**.

**Flights of foreign carriers and carriers from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan whose origin or stopover is within the territory also apply.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This regulation gives only the "general rules"; the concrete numbers are elsewhere.**
>
> Article 6 requires carriers to **formulate and publish "General Conditions of Carriage"**; Article 8 lists what they **must at least include**
> (refund/change rules, standards for special passengers, **baggage requirements**, oversale handling, complaint channels);
> Article 54 requires the General Conditions to be **filed on the civil-aviation service-quality supervision platform**.
>
> **In other words**: concrete numbers such as baggage size, weight and free allowance
> **are not in this regulation — they are in each airline's General Conditions of Carriage**.
>
> This is the same structure as `ev-043` (Beijing subway): **the regulation/rules give the general rule; the details are listed separately.**

## Usage

### What this regulation can and cannot answer

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| passengers' **statutory rights** (time limits and fees for refund, change, oversale, complaint) | "**how much my ticket** can be refunded" — that depends on the **ticket conditions** |
| what a carrier **must** do and **must not** do | the concrete size of "**whether this bag** can go on board" — that depends on **that airline's General Conditions** |
| **which version is in force** (has a document number, an effective date, and states which old rules it repealed) | "how a given airline actually performs" — the repo has no performance data |

### Three checks before use

| # | What to check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **whether it has been revised** | the repo records the version **effective 2021-09-01**, and the **page has no version number** (same problem as `ev-043`) |
| 2 | **whether what you ask is in its scope** | it is a **passenger-service** regulation; **safety** matters (dangerous goods, security) have separate regulations |
| 3 | **whether you should check the airline's General Conditions** | for **concrete numbers**, the regulation only requires "must be published" — **the numbers are the airline's** |

## Argument

### R4 · Why it is worth holding as a resource (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this regulation is worth holding in the repo's resource layer
- **Premise**: it is a **Transport Ministry Order** (a ministry-level regulation) with a document number, an index number, publication and effective dates, published on a **government information-disclosure page**, and it **explicitly states which three old rules it repealed** (ev-044)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. It meets the `资源` threshold: **official locator + accessible + clauses citable**
  2. It is a ready source of a criterion for **"what the repo should hold"**: the regulation **itself** requires airports to put up **signage guidance** at key areas of the boarding process (Article 28) —
     **i.e. process information is guaranteed to be visible on-site by law**
  3. By contrast, its **substantive rights clauses** (refund fees, oversale, complaint time limits)
     have **no mechanism guaranteeing passengers know them** — **that is the scarce information**
- **Evidence**: ev-044
- **Attacked**: **the repo cannot verify whether this page has been revised** (same as `ev-043`).
  And this entry's "repealed three old rules at once" point **itself shows that such documents are replaced wholesale** —
  **the repo has no version-detection mechanism** (see the section recorded in [命题：携带物品的尺寸与重量上限](../命题/携带物品的尺寸与重量上限.md)).
