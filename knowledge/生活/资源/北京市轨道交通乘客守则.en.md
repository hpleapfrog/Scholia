## Usage

### Three things to check before use

| # | What to check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **whether the page is the latest version** | the rules **get revised**. The repo records "the version effective 2021-09-28" — **that date is written by the page itself** |
| 2 | **whether the clause you ask about is in these rules** | the "riding rules" page also lists **10 fare documents**; **fares, discounts, card refunds are not in these rules** |
| 3 | **it is a normative document, not teaching material** | the rules say "**shall not**…" "**shall**…". **They give no reasons** and no teaching advice |

### What this document can and cannot support

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| "**what the official document stipulates**" — checkable clause by clause | "**why it is so stipulated**" — the rules give no reasons |
| "**when it took effect**" | "**whether it is still in force**" — the repo **cannot verify the page version** |
| concrete numbers (e.g. size/weight limits) | "**how well it is enforced**" — there is no enforcement or ridership data |

## Argument

### R3 · Why it is worth holding as a resource (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this official document is worth holding in the repo's resource layer
- **Premise**: it is a normative document **issued by the Beijing Municipal Transport Commission and published on the Beijing Subway official site**, with a clear effective date and a clickable official address (ev-043)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. It meets the `资源` threshold: **official locator + accessible + clauses citable**
  2. It answers a class of **repeatedly asked questions** (can I bring X, can I bring a pet, does a child need a ticket) — **this class has a unique correct answer, and the answer changes with the version**
  3. **"a unique correct answer that changes" is exactly why this repo exists**: online, answers to these questions are abundant and contradict each other
- **Evidence**: ev-043
- **Attacked**: **the repo cannot verify this page is the currently valid version** —
  the rules themselves carry **no revision number**, and the page only gives the effective date.
  So this entry can only claim "**the version effective 2021-09-28 was so**",
  **not "it is still so now**". This layer is written into the "Usage" section above.
