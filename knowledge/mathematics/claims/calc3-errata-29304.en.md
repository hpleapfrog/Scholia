## Statement

The public errata record [#29304](https://openstax.org/errata/29304) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2026-04-27; status Reviewed;
resolution Approved):

> In the proof of Thm 6.12 Green's Thm on a rectangle, on pg 638 of the pdf, there is a calculation that starts with $\int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}=$... On lines 3 and 4 of that calculation the parameterizations $r_1$, $r_2$, $r_3$, and $r_4$ should all be differentiated.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change, and the text will be updated in the next print cycle.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
