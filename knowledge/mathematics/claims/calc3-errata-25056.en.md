## Statement

The public errata record [#25056](https://openstax.org/errata/25056) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.7 Stokes’ Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2024-05-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Issue: No orientation is given for the surface.
> Priority: Type 3
> Fix: Add ", oriented upward" after "z≥0".

Publisher's resolution:

> This assessment will be updated.
