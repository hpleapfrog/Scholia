## 陈述

OpenStax《Calculus Volume 3》的公开勘误记录 [#24962](https://openstax.org/errata/24962) 针对 **Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** 记录如下
（提交 2024-05-07；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> Issue: The notation ∮_C is never introduced or defined. It starts being used randomly in the 6.3 Exercises, and then in Section 6.4, Theorem 6.12 (Green's Theorem, Circulation Form). It seems the author intended to use ∮_C when a line integral is calculated on a closed curve C, but the text is not consistent on that. Also, in Section 6.3, ∫_C was used for every curve C, regardless of whether it was closed.
> Priority: Type 3
> Fix: I suggest always using ∫_C , no matter the situation. Otherwise, have the author clarify the situations in which they intended to use ∮_C , and then make the text consistent.
