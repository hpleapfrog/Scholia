## Statement

The public errata record [#19876](https://openstax.org/errata/19876) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-30; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Example 6.46 asks for the calculation of a particular integral. In the very first line of the solution, the integral is reprinted -- but the integral in the solution is not the same as the integral in the question. In particular, the terms "x^3" and "y^3" switch places in the integrand. The integral in the question should be changed to match the integral in the solution.
>
> This error was pointed out in erratum 11448 back in 2020, and it was incorrectly marked as not an error; then, in erratum 19018, it was pointed out that the previous erratum was closed incorrectly, and the report was marked as a duplicate (!!!).……

Publisher's resolution:

> This example will be updated.

> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
