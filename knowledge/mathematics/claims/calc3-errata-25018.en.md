## Statement

The public errata record [#25018](https://openstax.org/errata/25018) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2024-05-14; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Issues: 
> The analogous equation is not Equation 6.20. It is Equation 6.7 from Theorem 6.3 in Section 6.2.
> The line integral equation should have a single integral, not a double integral.
> Priority: Type 1
> Fix: 
> Replace "6.20" with "6.7", and have the link direct to Equation 6.7.
>  Use a single integral:
> ∫_C f(x,y,z)ds=∫_a^b f(r(t)) ||r′(t)|| dt

Publisher's resolution:

> This paragraph will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
