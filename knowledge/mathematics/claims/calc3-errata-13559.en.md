## Statement

The public errata record [#13559](https://openstax.org/errata/13559) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2020-10-01; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Problem 304 seems like it belongs in Section 6.8, it's an impossible integral to do without the divergence theorem. I didn't check carefully, but this could also be an issue with 303 and 305.
>
> Also in 304, it's ambiguous whether the z is included in the arctangent on the j coordinate, maybe some parenthesis?

Publisher's resolution:

> In exercise 304, revise "xz" to "(xz)". Our reviewers determined this exercise should remain in section 6.6 as a more difficult exercise.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
