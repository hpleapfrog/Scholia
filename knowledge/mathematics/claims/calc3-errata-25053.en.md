## Statement

The public errata record [#25053](https://openstax.org/errata/25053) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.7 Stokes’ Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2024-05-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Issue: Everywhere else in the text refers to the "first" octant, rather than the "positive" octant.
> Priority: Type 4
> Fix: Use "first octant", and reword the last part of the sentence:
> " and S is the part of the plane x+y+z=1 oriented upward in the first octant: x≥0,y≥0,z≥0. "

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "positive" to "first."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
