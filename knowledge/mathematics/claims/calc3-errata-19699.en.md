## Statement

The public errata record [#19699](https://openstax.org/errata/19699) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.7 Stokes’ Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-12; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The answer key says "Both integrals give −136/45." Actually, the vector field is conservative, so the line integral around the simple closed, piecewise smooth boundary curve equals 0. This can be checked directly by parametrizing the boundary curve. So both integrals equal 0, not -136/45. 
>
> In addition, there are typos right at the beginning of the solution to Example 6.73. It reads "g(x,y)=4−x2−y2,gx=−2y...". While the calculations in the rest of the solutions are correct, the partial derivative of g with respect to g is -2x. It is the partial derivative of g with respect to y that equals -2y. The rest of the solution to this problem, while it skips some important steps, looks ok.

Publisher's resolution:

> The solutions will be updated.
