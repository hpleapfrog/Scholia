## Statement

The public errata record [#23372](https://openstax.org/errata/23372) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2023-11-16; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Issues: 
>  The cone equation is incorrect. The sides of the cone z=√(x^2+y^2 ) make a well-defined angle of ϕ_0=π/4 with the z-axis. It doesn't make sense to then "suppose" something about that angle.
>  The term "vertex angle" is not defined in the text, and its usage here does not match the common definition. It usually means the angle between two sides in a cross section, but this exercise takes it to mean the angle between the sides and the z-axis.
>  The answer is incorrect. 
> Fix:
>  Replace " z=√(x^2+y^2 ) " with " z=□cot (ϕ_0 ) √(x^2+y^2 ) ".
>  Replace " vertex angle of the cone " with " angle between the sides of the cone and the z-axis ".
>  Update the answer to be: (πa^7)/24 〖□sin〗^6 (ϕ_0 )

Publisher's resolution:

> This question will be updated.
