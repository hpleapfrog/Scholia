## Statement

The public errata record [#14294](https://openstax.org/errata/14294) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Chapter Review Exercises** and states the following (submitted 2020-11-19; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Exercise 449 in the Chapter 6 Review Exercises is ambiguous since we don't know which semi-circle to integrate along. If one assumes the upper semi-circle ($y\geq 0$) then the answer should be $\sqrt{2}(\pi +2\sqrt{2})$ which is different from the answer in the back of the book.

Publisher's resolution:

> In the stem, revise "a semicircle" to "an upper semicircle". Revise the answer to "sqrt2(2sqrt2 + π) = 4 + πsqrt2".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
