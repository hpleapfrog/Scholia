## Statement

The public errata record [#25017](https://openstax.org/errata/25017) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2024-05-14; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Issue: The final answer is incorrect. It is missing a minus sign. It could also be simplified a bit.
> Priority: Type 1
> Fix: Add the missing minus sign, and simplify the leading coefficient.
> =2π[1/64 (2√(4x^2+1) (8x^3+x)-〖□sinh〗^(-1) (2x))]_0^b 
>  =π/32 (2√(4b^2+1) (8b^3+b)-〖□sinh〗^(-1) (2b))

Publisher's resolution:

> A minus sign will be added.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
