## Statement

The public errata record [#13316](https://openstax.org/errata/13316) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2020-09-17; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Exercise 6.4.169, there's a missing \leq in the parameter range. Should be $-\sqrt{3} \leq t \leq \sqrt{3}$.

Publisher's resolution:

> This issue was addressed in another report and is correct in webview.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
