## Statement

The public errata record [#12643](https://openstax.org/errata/12643) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.1 Vector Fields** and states the following (submitted 2020-07-23; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Error in CHECKPOINT 6.10
> Verify that f(x,y)=x^2y^2+x.
> It should be f(x,y)=x^3y^2+x. Otherwise you are asking student to verify a false statement.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "x^2" to "x^3".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
