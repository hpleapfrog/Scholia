## Statement

The public errata record [#14995](https://openstax.org/errata/14995) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2021-01-28; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The PDF ( and online ) file lacks the integral you wish the students to evaluate. 
> After "Evaluate " you need to add
>
> $ \int\int_{S} ( x^2 + y - z) dS $ . The problem can then continue on until you have " $0 \le v \le 1$. " And then you need to delete the extra crud after that, which seems to be left over LaTeX from another problem; " for $ 0 \le u \le 1$, $ 0 \le v \le 2 $.

Publisher's resolution:

> This question will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
