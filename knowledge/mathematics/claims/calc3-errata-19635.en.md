## Statement

The public errata record [#19635](https://openstax.org/errata/19635) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> When parameterizing surfaces of revolution, the parametrization given is: r(x,θ)=⟨x,f(x)cosθ,f(x)sinθ⟩,a≤x≤b,0≤x<2π.
> θ should range from 0 to 2π, not x.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "x" to "θ".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
