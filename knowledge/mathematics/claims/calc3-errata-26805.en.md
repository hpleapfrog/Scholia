## Statement

The public errata record [#26805](https://openstax.org/errata/26805) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.8 The Divergence Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2025-03-07; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> There are two notations for a Flux integral (see equation (6.20) in section 6.6), but in this exercise they are mixed together. It is written 
> \iint_{\mathcal{S}} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{N} d\mathbf{S}
> Here either the \mathbf{N} must disappear, or the dS must not be bold. 
> So correct to either 
> \iint_{\mathcal{S}} \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S}
> (as used in the exercises 385, 387 and others around this one), or to 
> \iint_{\mathcal{S}} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{N} dS

Publisher's resolution:

> This question will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
