## Statement

The public errata record [#24461](https://openstax.org/errata/24461) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Chapter Review: Key Equations** and states the following (submitted 2024-03-28; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The first integrals listed in the entry for "Calculating a vector line integrals is listed as "int_C F dot ds" but this should be "int_C F dot dr"
>
> The entry for the flux form of Green's Theorem is not the flux form, it's just alternate notation for the circulation form.

Publisher's resolution:

> These will both be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
