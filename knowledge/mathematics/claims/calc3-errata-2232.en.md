## Statement

The public errata record [#2232](https://openstax.org/errata/2232) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6: Vector Calculus, Section: Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2016-12-08; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Formula has duplicate text
> ? S f(x, y, z)dSf(x, y, z)dS = ?D f(r(u, v)) ? t u t v ? dA.
>
> Should be:
> ?S f(x, y, z)dS = ?D f(r(u, v)) ? t u t v ? dA.
>
>
> The next formula directly after has the same error:
> ?C f(x, y, z)dsf(x, y, z)ds = ?ab f(r(t)) ? r?(t) ? dt.
>
> Should be:
> ?C f(x, y, z)ds = ?ab f(r(t)) ? r?(t) ? dt.
>
>
> This error is also in the web version.
>
> Thanks!
> Jacob Draddy

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
