## Statement

The public errata record [#19549](https://openstax.org/errata/19549) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.2 Line Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2022-04-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> I believe there are a few typos in this problem.
> In particular, the notation switches from "ds" to "dr" and back again, without explanation.
> Furthermore, in the calculation of C1, the bound switches from [0,2] to [0,1] and back again. I believe it should be [0,2].

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the upper limit in the second integral to "2".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
