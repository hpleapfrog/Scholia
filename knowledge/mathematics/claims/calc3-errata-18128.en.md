## Statement

The public errata record [#18128](https://openstax.org/errata/18128) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.3 Conservative Vector Fields** and states the following (submitted 2021-11-11; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In the statement of the FTC, the antiderivative F in the displayed formula should be italicized. Also, in Example 6.28, the dot should be aligned at the middle of the line (currently it is placed as if it were a period at the end of a sentence).

Publisher's resolution:

> Italicize "F" as indicated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
