## Statement

The public errata record [#29316](https://openstax.org/errata/29316) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2026-04-29; status Reviewed;
resolution Approved):

> In the second paragraph under checkpoint 6.37, f is a potential of the vector field F = <P,Q>. It is stated that f_x = P and f_x = Q. I think Q should be f_y, otherwise the gradient(f) = F wouldn't be correct. And furthermore the following sentence states f_xx = P_x and f_yy = Q_y which is only valid when Q = f_y so after taking the partial derivative with respect to y you get Q_y = f_yy.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change, and the text will be updated in the next print cycle.

> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
