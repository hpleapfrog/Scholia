## Statement

The public errata record [#19636](https://openstax.org/errata/19636) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> One of the steps in finding the surface area of the surface is to find the magnitude of the cross product, but the symbol for magnitude is missing when taking the norm of the cross product. The symbol should be added.

Publisher's resolution:

> The solution will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
