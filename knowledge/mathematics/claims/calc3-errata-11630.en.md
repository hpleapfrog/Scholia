## Statement

The public errata record [#11630](https://openstax.org/errata/11630) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6.7 at page 792** and states the following (submitted 2020-04-06; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> tx · ty = 〈 −gx, −gy, 1 〉 is more likely to be tx X ty (ie a cross product instead of a dot product)

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "tx · ty" to "tx × ty".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
