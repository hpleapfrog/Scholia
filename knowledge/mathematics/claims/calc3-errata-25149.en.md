## Statement

The public errata record [#25149](https://openstax.org/errata/25149) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.5 Divergence and Curl** and states the following (submitted 2024-05-29; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Issue: The answer is incorrect. (I think the author(s) intended to put this exercise after the instructions for 239−241, but since they didn't, students won't know what r is.)
> Priority: Type 1
> Fix: Update the answer to be: div F =2(x^2+y^2+z^2 )

Publisher's resolution:

> This assessment will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
