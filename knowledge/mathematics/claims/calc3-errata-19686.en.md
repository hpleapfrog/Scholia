## Statement

The public errata record [#19686](https://openstax.org/errata/19686) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.5 Divergence and Curl** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-10; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In the introduction of curl, the definition assumes that the partials P_x, Q_y, and R_z exist (which is the assumption for divergence, not curl). For curl, this should be the other partials: P_y, P_z, Q_x, Q_z, R_x, and R_y.

Publisher's resolution:

> The definition will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
