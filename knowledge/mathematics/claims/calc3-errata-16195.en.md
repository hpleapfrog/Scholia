## Statement

The public errata record [#16195](https://openstax.org/errata/16195) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.8 The Divergence Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2021-04-17; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> You corrected question 385 in Chapter 6.8 from 1/3 \pi to 4/3 \pi. This is because ( I assume ) you decided that the surface S of the hemisphere includes the bottom disk in the z=0 plane. This is an assumption that needs to be stated, otherwise the question is poorly defined. If S includes the bottom disk then the correction is valid and the solution is 4/3 \pi. If S does not include the bottom disk ( as is typically assumed in such questions) then you need to subtract the surface integral over the bottom disk, which is \pi, and hence the original solution was correct and should revert back to 1/3 \pi.……

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "4pi/3" to "pi/3".
