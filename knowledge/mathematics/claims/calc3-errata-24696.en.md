## Statement

The public errata record [#24696](https://openstax.org/errata/24696) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.7 Stokes’ Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2024-04-19; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The example is missing a choice of orientation for the surface. The vertices are given in nonconsecutive order, so the order of the vertices does not imply an orientation. It is better if the last two vertices are swapped: "C is the boundary of the parallelogram with vertices (0,0,1), (0,1,0), (2,1,−2), and (2,0,−1), traversed in this order."

Publisher's resolution:

> This example will be updated.
