## Statement

The public errata record [#14181](https://openstax.org/errata/14181) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2020-11-12; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The answer for Exercise 6.4.155 is incorrect. It should be $-20\pi$. Also, the integral shown in the answer is unrelated to the one given in the exercise and should be modified or removed.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "x^3dy" to "x^3ydy". Also revise the answer to "−20π".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
