## 陈述

OpenStax《Calculus Volume 3》的公开勘误记录 [#28405](https://openstax.org/errata/28405) 针对 **Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** 记录如下
（提交 2025-11-20；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> The error is in lack of attention to whether S should be boldface or not. The use of boldface is correct in Definition (6.20), but following it there are times when S (or dS) is rendered with the S in boldface though it should not be. For instance, it should not be bold when it appears in a flux integral about 6 lines below the definition, nor 3 lines further down as the start to a string of equalities. It should not be boldface in Equation (6.21), nor in the instructions for Example 6.70. I think it would be correct 6 lines below Checkpoint 6.59 following the words "Flow rate of fluid across S" if the other factor in the dot product were not missing.

出版方处理意见：

> Our reviewers accepted this change, and the text will be updated in the next print cycle.
