## Statement

The public errata record [#28406](https://openstax.org/errata/28406) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.7 Stokes’ Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2025-11-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The setup and figure have the surface being the graph of z = f(x,y). But following the figure, things have been renamed to z = g(x,y). It appears in the parametrization of the surface, in the tangent vectors, and text that follows over the next 6 lines or so.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change, and this figure and the text before the figure will be updated in the next print cycle.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
