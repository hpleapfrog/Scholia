## Statement

The public errata record [#17952](https://openstax.org/errata/17952) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2021-10-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The notation for a flux integral if you include an explicit normal vector N often has the wrong dS behind it. In the definition of formula (6.20) it is written correctly (green box in screenshot): After the bold N you get italic dS.
>
> There are four locations where it is dS with bold, upright capital S.
> All four of these S's should be changed to italic capital S. Alternatively you can remove the bold capital N from these formulas. 
> The alternative seems especially attractive in the second instance as you would get a trivial identity.
>
> (Note that the two cases with lower case ds in this text are written correctly as they are a different type of integral).

Publisher's resolution:

> Set "S" in italics and remove bold formatting.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
