## Statement

The public errata record [#18234](https://openstax.org/errata/18234) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2021-11-28; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The phrase "where S is the triangular region cut off from plane x+y+z=1 by the positive coordinate axes." does not make sense. Instead, the question should say that S is "the triangular portion of the plane x + y + z = 1 that lies in the first octant" or something like that. Also, S should be in italics or math mode.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "triangular region cut off from plane x + y + z = 1 by the positive coordinate axes" to "triangular region of the plane x + y + z = 1 in the first octant".
>
> Our reviewers determined that currently the text does not use italics or bold to identify planes, but this will be considered for future editions of the book.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
