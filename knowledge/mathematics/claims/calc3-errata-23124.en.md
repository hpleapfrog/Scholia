## Statement

The public errata record [#23124](https://openstax.org/errata/23124) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.1 Vector Fields** and states the following (submitted 2023-10-15; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> From the statement of the theorem, "Let F be a vector field in two or three dimensions such that the component functions of F have continuous second-order mixed-partial derivatives on the domain of F." 
>
> The proof of the theorem uses that the potential function for F has continuous mixed derivatives when it uses Clairaut's theorem (theorem 4.5 in section 4.3). And so what's needed is that the first derivatives of the components of F are continuous, not the second derivatives.……

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "second-order mixed-partial derivatives" to "first-order partial derivatives".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
