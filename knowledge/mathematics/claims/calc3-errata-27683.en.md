## Statement

The public errata record [#27683](https://openstax.org/errata/27683) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** and states the following (submitted 2025-08-27; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The derivative of x^2sin(theta) is x^2cos(theta), not -x^2cos(theta).

Publisher's resolution:

> The text will be updated in the next print cycle.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
