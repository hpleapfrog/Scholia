## Statement

The public errata record [#20192](https://openstax.org/errata/20192) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2022-08-01; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> If you want this to correspond to the given solution, the integral should be:
> (sin x - y^3 / 3) dx + (x^3 / 3 + sin y) dy

Publisher's resolution:

> The integral will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
