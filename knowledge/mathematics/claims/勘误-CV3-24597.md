## 陈述

OpenStax《Calculus Volume 3》的公开勘误记录 [#24597](https://openstax.org/errata/24597) 针对 **Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.6 Surface Integrals** 记录如下
（提交 2024-04-11；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> This section seems to have many errors/inconsistencies when it comes to units and terminology between "rate of flow" and "flux".
>
> (1) "The rate of flow, measured in mass per unit time per unit area, is ρN." If you are referring to the rate of flow, should this not be measured in units of distance per unit time? Also, why is density being multiplied by the normal? I believe what you meant to to say here is: "The mass flux, measured in mass per unit time per unit area, is ρv." What comes after then makes sense, as you are explaining how to calculate this mass flux by chopping up S into pieces and then calculating ρv*N to get the mass flux crossing S.……

出版方处理意见：

> Our reviewers determined the first two issues will be corrected. The third suggestion is something that will be considered for future editions of this book.
