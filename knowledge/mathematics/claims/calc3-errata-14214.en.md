## Statement

The public errata record [#14214](https://openstax.org/errata/14214) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.3 Conservative Vector Fields** and states the following (submitted 2020-11-14; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Calculus 3. Chapter 6. Section 3. Example 6.32. F(x,y) should be F(x,y,z) to reflect that the vector field is in R^3.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "(x, y)" to "(x, y, z)".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
