## Statement

The public errata record [#22172](https://openstax.org/errata/22172) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.5 Divergence and Curl** and states the following (submitted 2023-05-11; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Question 246 is suppose to align with question 251 but there is a typo. Question 246 should be "+xk" rather than "+zk" which is there now. With this change, the solution needs to be checked in the solution manual also. It should be 7 with the typo fixed.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "zk" to "xk".
