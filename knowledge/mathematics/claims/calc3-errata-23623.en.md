## Statement

The public errata record [#23623](https://openstax.org/errata/23623) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2023-12-28; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> I believe that the graph of the curve and the area given in the answer to Checkpoint 6.35 are both incorrect.
>
> The given answer is and area of 4/3 and I believe the correct area of this region is 2/3 sq. units.
> Also the graph should be symmetric about the y-axis, but it is shown to look different to the left of the y-axis than it does to the right of the y-axis.
>
> See my attached image of the plot I get for this curve.

Publisher's resolution:

> This checkpoint will be updated.
