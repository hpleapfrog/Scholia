## Statement

The public errata record [#18048](https://openstax.org/errata/18048) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.3 Conservative Vector Fields** and states the following (submitted 2021-11-02; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In the answer, it has a variable r instead of a constant 4 after doing integration by parts.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "1/r" to "1/4".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
