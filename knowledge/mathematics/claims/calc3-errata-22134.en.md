## Statement

The public errata record [#22134](https://openstax.org/errata/22134) for OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* concerns
**Chapter 6 Vector Calculus: Section 6.4 Green’s Theorem** and states the following (submitted 2023-05-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> This exercise is not solvable with Green's Theorem as stated. More information is needed about the differential ds. Computing the line integral around the four parts of the square does not give the answer of -5/6 that the solution shows. The line integral gives the value of 1/2 from my computation. The simplest way to correct this and keep the answer of -5/6 would be to express it as the line integral of (x^2y - 2xy + y^2)dx + (x^2y - 2xy + y^2)dy. The assumption of the existing problem is that ds = dx + dy which is not accurate or understood.

Publisher's resolution:

> Add the following to the end of the question stem " Use ds = dx + dy for this exercise."
