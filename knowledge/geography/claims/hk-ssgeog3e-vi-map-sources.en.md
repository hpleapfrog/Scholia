## Statement

《高中活學地理（第三版）》 (reprinted with corrections 2024), the "About this book" page (p.vi), **lacks a map-provenance note**.

Per the OUP official errata, the following should be added: the maps' China national boundary, province boundaries and affiliated islands
were drawn with reference to the Standard Map of the Ministry of Natural Resources of the PRC, with map-approval numbers
**GS (2023) 2762** and **GS (2023) 2766**.
