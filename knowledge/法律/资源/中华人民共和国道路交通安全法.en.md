## How to use

### Three things to check before using it

| # | Check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Is this the newest version** | the law has been amended three times. This repo records the **third-amendment version**, and **that version information comes from the page's own history** |
| 2 | **Range and specific standard are two layers** | the law often gives only a **range** (e.g. "five to fifty yuan"); **the specific enforcement standard is set by the provincial people's congress standing committee within that range** (Article 123) — see [specific enforcement standards are set by provincial standing committees](../命题/具体执行标准由省级人大常委会规定.en.md) |
| 3 | **It is a statute, not a case** | the law states "shall" / "shall not" and legal effects. **This repo takes neither case outcomes nor legal advice** (see the [law README](../README.en.md) boundary) |

### What this text can and cannot support

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| "**how the regime is laid down**" — elements, periods, legal effects, allocation of authority, checkable article by article | "**how a court would rule**" — the statute gives no outcomes |
| "**what a number is**" — fine ranges, periods, percentages, all explicit | "**what it actually is locally**" — the specific standard lives in provincial rules; the law only sets the range |
| "**which authority holds this power**" | "**how it is enforced**" — no enforcement data at all |

## Argument

### R3 · Why it is worth entering the resource layer (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this statute's official text deserves a place in the repo's resource layer
- **Premise**: it is a law enacted by the NPC Standing Committee; the official page carries an index
  number and a publication date; this repo read it through to Article 124 (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. It meets the `资源` threshold: **official locator + reachable + citable clauses** — the same class as
     [the Beijing Subway Passenger Rules](../../生活/资源/北京市轨道交通乘客守则.en.md)

     "by what authority" for a road rule ends here
  3. **"one correct answer, and it changes" is exactly why this repo exists**: what people say online
     about "how big is the fine" / "can I refuse to pay" is abundant and contradictory
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: **this repo cannot verify that this page is the currently effective version** —
  the page has **no revision number**, only a publication date and the amendment history.
  So the most this entry can claim is "**the version amended on 2021-04-29 says so**",
  **not "it still holds now"**. That limit is already carried in "How to use" above.
