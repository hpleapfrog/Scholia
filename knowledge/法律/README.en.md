# Law

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This subject holds "how statute law stipulates things", not "how this case was decided", and not "what you should do".**
>
> What goes in is **the institution itself**: which institution a statute establishes, what the
> constituent elements are, how long the period runs, to whom the legal effect attaches, which
> authority holds the power — **every item checkable against a specific clause**.
> **Neither case outcomes nor legal opinions are held**: the former are not statutory text, the latter are advice.

## Boundary

| | Holds | Does not hold |
|---|---|---|
| content | constituent elements, periods, legal effects and allocation of powers in laws, administrative regulations and judicial interpretations that are **checkable item by item** | case outcomes, legal opinions and advisory conclusions, "how to assert your rights" guides |
| source | the official text of a statute, with a **version and effective date** | repost pages, law-firm commentary, self-media summaries |
| phrasing | "Article N of the Law on X stipulates Y (version · effective date · clause no. to be verified)" | "you should do Y" / "courts usually rule Y" |

**Clause numbers are always marked as to-be-verified** — this repo does not write them from
memory, the same rule as [Transport](../交通/README.en.md).

**One statute may support several subjects.** One entry goes into one subject only;
if a concrete entry ever forces the issue, what changes is this section, not the entry.

### Timeliness: statutes get amended

After an amendment, an old entry's conclusion no longer holds. The handling matches
[Economics](../经济/README.en.md): **an entry records "what version N stipulated"**,
and when a newer version arrives the old entry moves to `已失效` (expired) **rather than being
rewritten in place** — so the reader always knows which version they are reading.

> [!NOTE]
> This repo has **no version-detection mechanism**, and `已失效` has never actually been
> triggered. That limitation is noted in [Daily life](../生活/README.en.md); this subject is
> bound by it too.
