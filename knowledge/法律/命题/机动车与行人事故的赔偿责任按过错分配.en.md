## Statement

Article 76 of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the PRC splits accident compensation into **three layers**:

| Layer | Who bears it |
|---|---|
| **Layer 1: compulsory motor-vehicle liability insurance** | the insurer compensates **within the limit of liability** of the compulsory third-party insurance |
| **Layer 2: the shortfall** | allocated under the rules below |
| **Layer 3: deliberate collision** | where the loss is caused by a pedestrian or non-motor-vehicle driver **deliberately colliding** with the vehicle, the motor-vehicle side bears **nothing** |

Layer 2 then splits into two situations:

| Situation | Allocation |
|---|---|
| **motor vehicle ↔ motor vehicle** | the **party at fault** bears it; if both are at fault, **in proportion to their respective fault** |
| **motor vehicle ↔ pedestrian / non-motor-vehicle driver** | if the pedestrian is not at fault, the **motor-vehicle side** bears it; if there is evidence the pedestrian was at fault, the motor-vehicle side's liability is **appropriately reduced according to the degree of fault**; **if the motor-vehicle side is not at fault, it bears no more than ten per cent** |

Original text (short quotes):

> 机动车一方没有过错的，承担不超过百分之十的赔偿责任。(Art. 76, para. 2, item 2)
>
> 交通事故的损失是由非机动车驾驶人、行人故意碰撞机动车造成的，机动车一方不承担赔偿责任。(Art. 76, para. 3)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **"Up to ten per cent even when not at fault" is the clause most often read past.**
>
> It is **not** "the pedestrian was entirely at fault so the vehicle pays nothing",
> nor "the vehicle always pays something". What the law gives is **a ceiling**:
> in the specific situation where **the motor-vehicle side is not at fault**,
> liability is **no more than ten per cent**. **A ceiling is not the same as that being the number.**

## Arguments

### A1 · The text itself writes down the allocation (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: the three-layer structure and the allocation in both situations are **the statute's explicit text**, not doctrine and not practice
- **Premise**: Article 76 first has the compulsory insurance pay, then allocates the shortfall in two ways, then exempts deliberate collision (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. This is "**to whom the legal effect attaches**" — exactly what the [law](../README.en.md) subject collects
  2. It is **falsifiable by the reader**: open the official page and search "第七十六条"
  3. **the insurance limit itself is another numeric system** (set by the State Council);
     this entry claims only the **order** — "the insurer pays first within the limit" — not what the limit is
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: see A2
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12

### A2 · This entry claims one version only, and is not an adjudication rule (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this entry claims "**the version amended on 2021-04-29 says so**", **not "it still holds now"**
- **Premise**: the official page carries **no revision number**, only a publication date and the three-amendment history (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. the law **has already been amended three times** — this class of text **does get replaced**
  2. and this repo has **no mechanism** to detect a further amendment
  3. writing "still holds now" into the statement would be **beyond the evidence**
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: **this entry has checked no lower-level rule, and no judgment document.**
  **How much** "appropriately reduced" reduces by, **what** the "no more than ten per cent" is in a
  concrete case, **how** "deliberately colliding" is found — **all of these are fact-finding and
  discretion, and this repo has looked at none of them.**
  So this entry can only support "**how the law allocates**", **not "what your case will be awarded"**.
  The latter needs case evidence, and the [law](../README.en.md) subject's boundary explicitly
  **takes neither case outcomes**.
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12
- **To fill in**: **the text setting the compulsory-insurance limit of liability** (set by the State Council, not yet registered here).
  With it, this proposition can fill in the **limit** inside "the insurer pays first within the limit".

## Why this counts as real information rather than noise

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **On "who pays whom, and how much", what people say online contradicts itself, while the law gives a structure checkable clause by clause.**
>
> What you can see on site is **the result** (a collision, a casualty);
> what the law writes down is a **three-layer structure** (compulsory insurance first → allocate by fault → deliberate collision exempt)
> plus **a ceiling that is often read past** (up to ten per cent when the motor-vehicle side is faultless).
>
> **This repo's mission is to strip noise and hold true information.**
> That contrast is the test: **what is visible on site, this repo need not repeat;
> what is invisible on site yet written in law is what belongs here.**
> "If the pedestrian was at fault, does the vehicle still pay" — the question has no answer on any sign.
