## Statement

Article 75 of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the PRC writes "treat first, settle later" into explicit text:

> 医疗机构对交通事故中的受伤人员应当及时抢救，不得因抢救费用未及时支付而拖延救治。(Art. 75, para. 1)

Who advances the rescue fee splits into three situations:

| Situation | Who advances first |
|---|---|
| the vehicle **has** compulsory third-party insurance | **the insurer pays within the limit of liability** |
| the rescue fee **exceeds that limit** | **the road-traffic accident social assistance fund** advances **part or all** of it |
| **no** compulsory insurance, or **the driver fled the scene** | as above — the **assistance fund** advances it |

And there is a right of recovery in the second paragraph that is easy to read past:

> 道路交通事故社会救助基金管理机构有权向交通事故责任人追偿。(Art. 75, para. 2)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **"May not delay treatment" binds the medical institution — not the casualty, and not the money.**
>
> This article separates the **duty to treat** from the **settlement of fees**: the duty comes first,
> settlement after, and **it explicitly forbids using "the money hasn't arrived" as a reason to delay**.
> The three fee situations are about **who advances** —
> **advancing is not the same as forgiving**, which is what the right of recovery at the end shows.

## Argument

### A1 · The text itself writes down the duty and the order of advancing (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: "may not delay treatment" is not a professional appeal, it is **an explicit statutory prohibition of omission**
- **Premise**: Article 75 para. 1 forbids, with "shall not", delaying treatment on the ground that fees are unpaid; para. 2 gives the three advancing situations and the fund's right of recovery (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. This is "**who must do what, to whom the obligation attaches, to whom the legal effect attaches**" — exactly what the [law](../README.en.md) subject collects
  2. It is **falsifiable by the reader**: open the official page and search "第七十五条"
  3. The three situations (insured / over the limit / uninsured or fled) are **exhaustive**, and the exhaustive enumeration is itself a checkable structure
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: see A2
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12

### A2 · This entry claims one version only, and has not checked enforcement (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: this entry claims "**the version amended on 2021-04-29 says so**", **not "it still holds now"**
- **Premise**: the official page carries **no revision number**, only a publication date and the three-amendment history (ev-051)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. the law **has already been amended three times** — this class of text **does get replaced**
  2. and this repo has **no mechanism** to detect a further amendment
  3. writing "still holds now" into the statement would be **beyond the evidence**
- **Evidence**: ev-051
- **Attacked by**: **this entry has checked neither the assistance fund's administrative measures nor any enforcement data.**
  "**Where the fund is, how to apply, how much it advances**" — **this repo has looked at none of it.**
  Likewise, "whether treatment is in practice never delayed" is an **enforcement** question:
  the law writes "shall", and **this repo has no data on how far that is enforced.**
  So this entry can only support "**what the law forbids, and the order in which money is advanced**",
  **not "in practice treatment is never delayed"**.
- **Signed**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-12
- **To be added**: **the administrative measures for the road-traffic accident social assistance fund** (not yet registered here).
  With it, this proposition can reach from "who shall advance" to "how it is actually advanced".

## Why this counts as real information rather than noise

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The saying "pay first, treat after" directly contradicts this article, and this article is explicit statutory text.**
>
> What you can see on site is **a rescue and a bill**;
> what the law writes down is **the order between duty and fees** —
> treatment comes first, and it is **explicitly forbidden** to put fee settlement ahead of treatment.
>
> **This repo's mission is to strip noise and hold true information.**
> That contrast is the test: **what is visible on site, this repo need not repeat;
> what is invisible on site yet written in law is what belongs here.**
> "If the money hasn't arrived, may they refuse to treat" — the question has no answer on any sign,
> while the law gives an explicit "shall not".
