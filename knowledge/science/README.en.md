## Why this erratum matters more than usual

> [!IMPORTANT]
> What's wrong is **not the wording but the data source**.
>
> A wording error is often self-evident to readers; **an unreliable source usually is not** —
> the student sees a distribution chart that looks perfectly normal.

This touches the repo's evidence-threshold principle directly:
**data whose source cannot be traced must not enter the judgment.**
