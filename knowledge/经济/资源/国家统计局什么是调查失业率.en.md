### What this document specifies

| Section | Content | Quotable short quote |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Concept | the rate = unemployed ÷ (employed + unemployed) × 100%; **the population aged 16 and over = labour force + non-labour-force** | "this classification is the **statistical standard recommended by the International Labour Organization**; the survey unemployment rate computed on it is **internationally comparable**" |
| 2 Statistical method | stratified, two-stage, probability-proportional-to-size sampling; **about 340,000 households surveyed each month** | "at the 90% confidence level, the **relative error of the national urban survey unemployment rate is kept within 2%**, and within 10% for each province" |
| 3 How to read it | **three usage warnings** (it cannot fall to zero / no necessary relation to growth / unsuitable for assessment) | see [The urban survey unemployment rate is unsuitable as an assessment basis and should not be cascaded down](../命题/城镇调查失业率不宜作为考核依据也不宜层层分解.en.md) |
| 4 Worked example | figures for specific periods, e.g. 5.2% in June 2023 | — |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The valuable part of this document is not the figures but the three usage warnings in section 3.**
>
> Figures are superseded by the next release; **"what this indicator must not be used for" is part of the
> basis, and does not change with the period.** This repo treats the two kinds separately: figures are
> archived per period, warnings stand as entries in their own right.

## How to use

### What this text can and cannot support

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| "**What are the three conditions for unemployment**" — spelled out one by one in section 1 | "**What is the unemployment rate now**" — section 4 gives June 2023, long superseded |
| "**How large is the sampling error**" — within 2% nationally and 10% per province at 90% confidence | "**How large is the error this period**" — that is a design figure, not a measurement for this period |
| "**May the rate be used as a performance target**" — section 3 says in terms that it may not | "**Does a place's rate show how well its employment work is going**" — section 3 says the significance at city and county level is limited |
| "**Why the rate cannot fall to zero**" — frictional + structural = the natural rate | "**What is the relation between the rate and GDP growth**" — section 2 says there is **no necessary rule** |
