## Statement

**Carriers, airports, ground handlers, sales agents and sales platforms must, within 10 working days of receiving a passenger complaint, produce a handling result containing a solution; if they decline to accept the complaint they must state their reasons.**

Article 52 of the 《公共航空运输旅客服务管理规定》 states (short quote):

> ……收到旅客投诉后，应当**及时受理**；**不予受理的，应当说明理由**。
> ……应当在**收到旅客投诉之日起 10 个工作日内**做出**包含解决方案的处理结果**。
> ……应当**书面记录**旅客的投诉情况及处理结果，**投诉记录至少保存 3 年**。

### Who you may complain to, and through what channels

Article 50 lists the recipients:

| Recipient | Source |
|---|---|
| The carrier | §50 |
| The airport operator | §50 |
| The ground handler | §50 |
| The sales agent | §50 |
| The online sales platform operator | §50 |
| **The civil aviation administrative authority** | §50 |

Article 51 adds three duties:

| Duty | Source |
|---|---|
| **Publish an email address and a complaints telephone number within the PRC** | §51 para. 1 |
| **Set up a dedicated body or designate dedicated staff to receive complaints** | §51 para. 2 |
| **Hong Kong / Macao / Taiwan carriers and foreign carriers must be able to receive and handle complaints in Chinese** | §51 para. 3 |

### The channel to the civil aviation authority

Article 53 names who runs it:

> **民航局消费者事务中心**受民航局委托统一受理旅客向民航行政机关的投诉。
> ……旅客向民航行政机关投诉的，……应当在**民航服务质量监督平台**上进行投诉处理工作。

### The consequence of breaching these two

Article 58 places "failing to receive or handle complaints as required" inside the scope of **an order to rectify within a time limit plus a record on the civil aviation industry's serious-dishonesty credit register**.

## Argument

### A1 · "10 working days" is written in the regulation and comes with three companion duties (not yet attacked)

- **Claim**: **producing a handling result containing a solution within 10 working days** is written in the regulation, and **"state reasons if declining" and "keep records for 3 years" are two more duties in the same article**
- **Premise**: the three sentences of Article 52 (ev-044)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. This is "what an official document specifies" — **the official text is the primary source**
  2. **"containing a solution" is a qualifier of "handling result"** — what the regulation demands is not "we received it" but **"here is what we intend to do"**
  3. **"if they decline, they must state reasons" closes the door on "decline without explaining"**
  4. Same shape as [involuntary refunds being free of charge](involuntary-refund-free.en.md): **a hard constraint with an explicit deadline, and a deadline most passengers do not know exists**
- **Evidence**: ev-044
- **Attacked by**: see A2

### A2 · "Accept promptly" has no deadline, and this entry covers only the latter half (not yet attacked)

- **Claim**: this entry's deadline applies only to **"producing the handling result"**; **not to the act of accepting the complaint**
- **Premise**: Article 52 para. 1 says "shall **accept promptly**", and **the regulation assigns no number of days to "promptly"** (ev-044)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. So "how soon counts as accepting" **is not answerable here** — **the regulation only fixed the 10 working days for the handling result**
  2. **10 working days exclude statutory holidays** — the regulation says "working days" not "days", **the same wording as the 7 working days for refunds (§26)**
  3. **§26 expressly excludes financial-institution processing time; §52 has no comparable deduction** — **§26's deduction rule cannot be carried over to §52**
- **Evidence**: ev-044
- **Attacked by**: **This entry states Articles 50–53 and 58 of the version in force since 2021-09-01 only; it does not guarantee the rule still stands.** The Ministry page **carries no version number** — **this repo has no mechanism to detect whether it has been amended**. Also: **this entry has not checked the CAAC Consumer Affairs Center's actual processing times**, nor **any carrier's actual response time** — **the gap between the regulation's deadline and actual practice, this repo has no data on.**
- **Author**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-13
- **To be added**: **a version-detection mechanism**; published turnaround data from the CAAC Consumer Affairs Center; a measurement of at least one carrier's actual complaint response time

## Why this counts as real information rather than noise

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Article 28 of the regulation requires airports to post signage at every key point of the boarding flow — so rules like "check-in → security → boarding" are guaranteed to be visible on site.**
>
> **"After you complain you are owed a reply containing a solution within 10 working days, and a decline must come with reasons" appears on no sign.** It even cuts against what the counter suggests: what a passenger usually gets is "we will look into it", **while the regulation demands "a solution within 10 working days".**
>
> **This library's mission is to remove noise and store real information.**
> What is visible on site, this repo need not repeat; **what is invisible on site and fixes an explicit deadline with consequences is exactly what this repo should hold.**
> This entry is the latter — **the sentence most easily talked past when you complain, and the one most useful for holding someone to it.**
