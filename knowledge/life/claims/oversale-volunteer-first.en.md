## Statement

《公共航空运输旅客服务管理规定》 provides that when oversold, the **volunteer-solicitation process must be run first**:

Original text (short quotations):

> When the number of passengers actually flying exceeds the number of seats due to overselling, the carrier or its ground-service agent shall, **under the volunteer-solicitation procedure**, seek passengers who voluntarily give up their trip.
> **Without the volunteer-solicitation procedure, the priority-boarding rule shall not be used to determine which passengers are denied boarding.** (§44)

> The carrier or its ground-service agent may, **only after the volunteer-solicitation procedure fails to find enough volunteers**,
> determine the denied-boarding passengers under the priority-boarding rule. (§46 para 2)

> The carrier or its ground-service agent shall, per the oversale-handling rules, **give compensation** to denied-boarding passengers and provide related services. (§47)

**The three supporting obligations:**

| Article | Requirement |
|---|---|
| §43 | the oversale-handling rules (including the **volunteer-solicitation procedure**, priority-boarding rule, compensation standards) must be written into the **General Conditions of Carriage** |
| §46 | the priority-boarding rule **shall conform to public order and good morals**, at least considering **the needs of special passengers (old, young, sick, disabled, pregnant) and connections to later flights** |
| §48 | on the passenger's request, the carrier **shall issue** a **certificate** of "gave up the trip or was denied boarding due to oversale" |
| §49 | changes or refunds due to oversale are handled **per §24 para 1 and §25** (i.e. **no fee**) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **"Without the volunteer-solicitation procedure, one shall not…" is a hard procedural constraint, not industry practice.**
>
> It means: **an airline cannot skip the "ask if anyone volunteers" step and directly name who to bump.**
> And **most passengers at the gate do not know they have the right to demand this step**.
>
> **The §48 "shall issue a certificate" obligation matters just as much** — it is the passenger's basis for later claiming compensation and refund/change,
> and **it is issued only on the passenger's request**.

## Argument

### A1 · "Shall not" is a checkable hard constraint (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: §44's "without the volunteer-solicitation procedure, the priority-boarding rule **shall not** be used to determine denied-boarding passengers"
  is a **prohibitive provision**, not an exhortation
- **Premise**: the sentence uses "shall not" (不得), and Article 59 lists violations of this regulation as
  **subject to a deadline order to correct, and to being recorded in the civil-aviation industry serious-credit-default record if overdue** (ev-044)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. "不得" in a regulation is prohibitive wording; this entry is not a paraphrase of industry practice
  2. **A supporting enforcement clause exists** — `§58` explicitly lists "failing to provide post-oversale service to passengers as required"
     among the acts subject to a correction order and possible serious-credit-default recording
- **Evidence**: ev-044
- **Attacked**: see A2
- **Attribution**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-05

### A2 · This entry does not declare "how it is actually performed" (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: what the regulation **writes** is what this entry can declare; **how well it is performed** is not
- **Premise**: the repo has only the regulation text, **no enforcement, complaint or case-law data** (ev-044)
- **Reasoning**:
  1. a regulation having a prohibitive clause **does not mean** every gate follows it
  2. the repo has **no channel** to obtain data on "whether a given airline's given oversale ran the solicitation process"
  3. so this entry **declares only the normative content** — the same discipline as the existing 教法 entry "textbook-specified ≠ measured effective"
- **Evidence**: ev-044
- **Attacked**: **this entry's bold wording ("cannot skip") may be read by passengers as "the airline definitely won't skip"** —
  that is **treating a norm as a fact**. The `Statement` is already limited to "the regulation provides",
  but **readers will likely remember only the bold half**.
- **Response**: **this risk is real.** That is why the entry's title reads
  "**no passenger may be denied boarding without the volunteer-solicitation process**" — **"shall not" is a normative word, not a factual word**.
  If readers are later found to misread it broadly, the title should become a question.
- **Attribution**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-05
