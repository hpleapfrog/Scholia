## Statement

The public errata record [#29363](https://openstax.org/errata/29363) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Chapter Review: Additional Problems** and states the following (submitted 2026-05-14; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Chapter 4 Additional Problem 91 has inconsistent unit vectors. The vector for acceleration (3.0i + 4.0k)m/s^2 should likely be (3.0i + 4.0j)m/s^2, with a j instead of a k. In the answers section for that chapter the correct unit vector j is listed, "(265.0i + 20.0j)m/s".

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change, and the text will be updated in the next print cycle.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
