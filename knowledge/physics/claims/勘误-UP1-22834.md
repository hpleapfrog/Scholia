## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#22834](https://openstax.org/errata/22834) 针对 **Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Chapter Review: Problems** 记录如下
（提交 2023-09-07；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> The problem says |A| = |B| = 5, |A+B| = 3, and |A-B| = 3, and it asks for the angle between A and B. That's contradictory: Given the magnitudes of both vectors and their sum, the problem can be solved with the law of cosines to give theta = arccos(-41/50), whereas given their difference, theta = arccos(+41/50). Another way to look at is that |A+B| = |A-B| only if the angle between them is 90°, or if either A or B = 0. (Succinctly, |A+B| = |A-B| iff A·B = 0). Some piece of information, such as |A-B|, needs to be removed from the problem.
