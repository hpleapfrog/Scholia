## Statement

The public errata record [#7032](https://openstax.org/errata/7032) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 4, problem 87** and states the following (submitted 2018-09-29; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The problem needs to say that at t=0, the particle is on the x axis and that it's moving counterclockwise in the xy plane (or around the z axis).

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "...t = 0 s." to "...t = 0 s where it is on the x-axis and moving counterclockwise in the xy plane."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
