## Statement

The public errata record [#19291](https://openstax.org/errata/19291) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Chapter Review** and states the following (submitted 2022-03-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> problem 57b asks for the tension between the ropes but instead we get acceleration as the answer

Publisher's resolution:

> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
