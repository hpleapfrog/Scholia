## Statement

The public errata record [#25899](https://openstax.org/errata/25899) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Answer Key: Chapter 3** and states the following (submitted 2024-10-10; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Chapter 3 question 29 part b asks us to compare the speed of the blast wave to the speed of sound. We are given 343 m/s as the speed of sound and the correct answer for the blast wave in part a is 902 m/s. However, the book answer for part b is only 163%. It should be 263% (also, 163% of 343 m/s is only 559 m/s.)

Publisher's resolution:

> The answer will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
