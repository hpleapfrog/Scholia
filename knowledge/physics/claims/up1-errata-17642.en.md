## Statement

The public errata record [#17642](https://openstax.org/errata/17642) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.5 Estimates and Fermi Calculations** and states the following (submitted 2021-09-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> A reference to significant figures is made without explaining what it is (which is explained in the next section). 
> "One “sig. fig.” is fine. There is no need to go beyond one significant figure when doing calculations to obtain an estimate. In most cases, the order of magnitude is good enough. The goal is just to get in the ballpark figure, so keep the arithmetic as simple as possible." Students do not know yet what sig fig is.
> - GM

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "There is no need to go beyond one significant figure when doing calculations to obtain an estimate." to "There is no need to go beyond one significant figure, or one digit in the coefficient of an expression in scientific notation, when doing calculations to obtain an estimate."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
