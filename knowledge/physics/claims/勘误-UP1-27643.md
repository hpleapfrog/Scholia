## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#27643](https://openstax.org/errata/27643) 针对 **Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.1 Position, Displacement, and Average Velocity** 记录如下
（提交 2025-08-13；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> The question asks for the 'final' displacement. The answer key deals with 'total' displacement. The total displacement is the sum of four displacement vectors. One of these is the initial (first) displacment. A different vector is the final (fourth) displacment.
>
> Consider using 'total' in the question, just as you do in the answer to subproblem 3.1 (b), in stead of 'final', for clarity.

出版方处理意见：

> Revise "final" to "total".
