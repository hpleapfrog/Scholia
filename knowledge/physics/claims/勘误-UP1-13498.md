## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#13498](https://openstax.org/errata/13498) 针对 **Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Chapter Review: Problems** 记录如下
（提交 2020-09-26；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> Problem 4.19 reads "The 18th hole at Pebble Beach Golf Course is a dogleg to the left of length 496.0 m. The fairway off the tee is taken to be the x direction. A golfer hits his tee shot a distance of 300.0 m, corresponding to a displacement...  What is the final displacement of the golf ball from the tee?"
>
> This assumes a level of familiarity with golf that some students do not have.  The required knowledge is that the tee shot originates from the tee.  Why not pose the question "What is the final displacement of the golf ball from where it started?"

出版方处理意见：

> Revise "from the tee" to "from where it started".
