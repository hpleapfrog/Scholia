## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#20547](https://openstax.org/errata/20547) 针对 **Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.2 Acceleration Vector** 记录如下
（提交 2022-09-12；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> There was an earlier errata that addressed this same figure, but I believe the caption for the figure is still incorrect. As best as I can tell, the role of the red stars and blue dots are reversed. The blue dots are the projection onto the xy plane, and the red stars are the actual positions in 3-D.

出版方处理意见：

> In the caption, revise "blue dots" to "red stars" and "red stars" to "blue dots".
