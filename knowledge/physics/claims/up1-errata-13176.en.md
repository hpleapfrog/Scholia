## Statement

The public errata record [#13176](https://openstax.org/errata/13176) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Section 5.6 Common Forces** and states the following (submitted 2020-09-10; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> During the calculation, assuming no friction, the first equation within the floating text on page 239 needs to be corrected. The minus sign should be an equal sign.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise " − mg" to "= mg".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
