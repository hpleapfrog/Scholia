## Statement

The public errata record [#23773](https://openstax.org/errata/23773) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.3 Unit Conversion** and states the following (submitted 2024-01-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Shouldn't it say "Given that 1 lbf (pound-force) is approximately 4.45 N, were the numbers being output by SM_FORCES too big or too small?" instead of "Given that 1 lb (pound) is 4.45 N, were the numbers being output by SM_FORCES too big or too small?"?

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "pound-seconds" to "pound-force-seconds", "lb-s" to "lbf-s", "pound" to "pound-force" and "lb" to "lbf".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
