## Statement

The public errata record [#1993](https://openstax.org/errata/1993) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 1: Units and Measurement, Section: The Scope and Scale of Physics** and states the following (submitted 2016-10-24; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> It now states that 1 mm = 1 cm.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
