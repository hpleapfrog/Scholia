## Statement

The public errata record [#12891](https://openstax.org/errata/12891) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.1 Position, Displacement, and Average Velocity** and states the following (submitted 2020-08-25; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In the calculation for the total displacement of two separate movements, the book says: "The total displacement is 2 − 4 = −2 m to the left, or in the negative direction."
> I suggest to change the answer to either -2 m along the x-axis or stating that it's 2 m to the left.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the sentence "The total displacement is..." to "The total displacement is 2 − 4 = −2 m along the x-axis."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
