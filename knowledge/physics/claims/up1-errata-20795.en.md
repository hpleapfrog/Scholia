## Statement

The public errata record [#20795](https://openstax.org/errata/20795) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.4 Uniform Circular Motion** and states the following (submitted 2022-10-09; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The first equation in the solution for Example 4.11 for the period T of circular motion is given as
>
> T = 2πr/v
>
> This comes from the definition of average speed as circumference divided by the time it takes to go in a circle:
>
> v = 2πr/T
>
> It seems obvious to a physics teacher where the equation comes from but as it comes almost immediately after period T is defined, students new to the material may need further explication as to the origins of T = 2πr/v.……

Publisher's resolution:

> The solution will be updated.
