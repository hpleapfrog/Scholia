## Statement

The public errata record [#18527](https://openstax.org/errata/18527) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Section 5.5 Newton’s Third Law** and states the following (submitted 2022-01-08; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In Figure 5.20, the forces F_foot and F_floor should be drawn with equal length, because they are a action-reaction force pair and hence are equal in magnitude. In the figure, F_floor is noticeably shorter (measuring their actual lengths, length of F_floor is only 75% of F_foot).
>
> While this isn't a serious error, it's the kind of detail that is nice to get right.

Publisher's resolution:

> This figure will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
