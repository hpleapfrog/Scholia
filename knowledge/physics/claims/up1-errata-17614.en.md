## Statement

The public errata record [#17614](https://openstax.org/errata/17614) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.1 The Scope and Scale of Physics** and states the following (submitted 2021-09-19; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The sentence, "The kinetic theory of gases, on the other hand, is a model in which a gas is viewed as being composed of atoms and molecules." which attempts to help students better understand what theories are needs to be removed. It "explains" one unknown (theories) in terms of another unknown (kinetic theory of gasses) from student perspective. - Gulmammad Mammadov

Publisher's resolution:

> Delete the sentences "The kinetic theory of gases, on the other hand, is a model in which a gas is viewed as being composed of atoms and molecules. Atoms and molecules are too small to be observed directly with our senses—thus, we picture them mentally to understand what the instruments tell us about the behavior of gases."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
