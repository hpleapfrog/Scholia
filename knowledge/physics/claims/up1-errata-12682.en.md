## Statement

The public errata record [#12682](https://openstax.org/errata/12682) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.4 Motion with Constant Acceleration** and states the following (submitted 2020-07-30; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Two problems:
> 1) Somehow, the variables for position, velocity, and acceleration need to be distinguished between the two animals (cheetah and gazelle). There should be subscripts to identify each animal. 
> 2) When numbers are plugged in at the end of step a and also in step b, the numbers do not include units. Units should be included, like they are earlier in this section in Example 3.12.

Publisher's resolution:

> Units will be added in the solution. Our reviewers decided not to add subscript labels on each equation.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
