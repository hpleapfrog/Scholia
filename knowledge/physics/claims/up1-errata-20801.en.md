## Statement

The public errata record [#20801](https://openstax.org/errata/20801) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.5 Relative Motion in One and Two Dimensions** and states the following (submitted 2022-10-09; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING 4.6
>
> A boat heads north in still water at 4.5 m/s directly across a river that is running east at 3.0 m/s. What is the velocity of the boat with respect to Earth?
>
> This should read:
>
> A boat that can travel at 4.5 m/s in still water heads north on a river that is running east at 3.0 m/s...

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "A boat heads due north, moving at 4.5 m/s directly across a river that is running east at 3.0 m/s." to "A boat heads north in still water at 4.5 m/s relative to the water of a river that is running east at 3.0 m/s."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
