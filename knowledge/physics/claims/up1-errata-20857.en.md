## Statement

The public errata record [#20857](https://openstax.org/errata/20857) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Section 2.3 Algebra of Vectors** and states the following (submitted 2022-10-17; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In the "solution" section of example 2.13:
>
> > "Therefore, his displacement vector D_TB along the stairs is"
>
> The vector should be D_AT, as that is the one going along the stairs, and is also the one being calculated in the equation that follows.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the subscript "TB" to "AT".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
