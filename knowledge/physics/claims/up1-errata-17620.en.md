## Statement

The public errata record [#17620](https://openstax.org/errata/17620) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.2 Units and Standards** and states the following (submitted 2021-09-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The sentence, "The length of the meter will change if the speed of light is someday measured with greater accuracy." is not needed. Now the length of meter is defined to be constant and from it the speed of light is derived as a constant with zero uncertainty. In other words, the speed of light is already, by definition, measured with infinitely high accuracy. - GM

Publisher's resolution:

> Delete the sentence "The length of the meter will change if the speed of light is someday measured with greater accuracy."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
