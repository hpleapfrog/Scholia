## Statement

The public errata record [#14972](https://openstax.org/errata/14972) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Chapter Review: Problems** and states the following (submitted 2021-01-24; status Completed;
resolution Approved):
