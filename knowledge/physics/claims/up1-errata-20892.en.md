## Statement

The public errata record [#20892](https://openstax.org/errata/20892) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.3 Projectile Motion** and states the following (submitted 2022-10-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The last sentence of Example 4.8, solution part (a), states:
> "Thus, any projectile that has an initial vertical velocity of 21.2 m/s and lands 10.0 m below its starting altitude spends 3.79 s in the air." 
> However, it should say that this is applicable for "any projectile that has an initial vertical velocity of 21.2 m/s and lands 10.0 m [above] its starting altitude" because that is what was solved for in the example.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "below" to "above".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
