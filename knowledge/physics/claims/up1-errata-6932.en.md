## Statement

The public errata record [#6932](https://openstax.org/errata/6932) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 5, problem 83** and states the following (submitted 2018-09-16; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The answer is inconsistent. The work shows the answer as 2mk^2x^3, but then reports it as 2mk^2x^2.
>
> "F=2mk2x2; First, take the derivative of the velocity function to obtain a=2kxv=2kx(kx2)=2k2x3. Then apply Newton’s second law F=ma=2mk2x2."

Publisher's resolution:

> This is corrected in webview and the solution guide.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
