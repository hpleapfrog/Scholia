## Statement

The public errata record [#25715](https://openstax.org/errata/25715) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.3 Average and Instantaneous Acceleration** and states the following (submitted 2024-09-11; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Example problem 3.6 has the wrong units for the functional form of the velocity. Should be:
> v(t)=(20 m/s/s) * t - (5 m/s^3)t^2.

Publisher's resolution:

> This example will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
