## Statement

The public errata record [#26738](https://openstax.org/errata/26738) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.2 Instantaneous Velocity and Speed** and states the following (submitted 2025-03-03; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The first entry states the speed of continental drift as 10^-7 m/s. If this were true, I estimated that the continents would be moving at about 3 meters per year, which seems totally untrue to me. I then looked up what the true speed of continental drift was, and found that it is about 2-10 cm/yr, or about 10^-9 m/s. Here are some sources:
> https://www.britannica.com/science/continental-drift-geology
> https://hypertextbook.com/facts/1997/ZhenHuang.shtml

Publisher's resolution:

> Change the superscripts to "-9" in the first row of the table.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
