## Statement

The public errata record [#2091](https://openstax.org/errata/2091) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 2: Vectors, End of Chapter Exercises** and states the following (submitted 2016-11-10; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In Chapter 2, Problems 52 and 53 both refer to a figure that is not there. This is also the case for Problem 60. (page 102)

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
