## Statement

The public errata record [#20194](https://openstax.org/errata/20194) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.6 Finding Velocity and Displacement from Acceleration** and states the following (submitted 2022-08-01; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Units are inconsistently specified in Example 3.17. While the unit for a(t) is correctly specified in the expression a(t)=-(1/4)t m/s^3 (imputing a time dimension to variable t, I think the submitter of errata 13235 was making this mistake, not realizing variable t has its own unit), in the remainder of the work, units are not consistently specified. For example, the line for obtaining v(t) by integration simply has no units, resulting in final answer of -(1/8)t^2+C1, where it should be -(1/8 m/s^3)*t^2+C1.……

Publisher's resolution:

> This example will be updated to add units throughout.
