## Statement

The public errata record [#4988](https://openstax.org/errata/4988) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 4 Displacement and Velocity Vectors (4.1)** and states the following (submitted 2018-01-18; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The first Example (Polar Orbiting Satellite) is cut short. Should be extended to include solution formulas and significance.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
