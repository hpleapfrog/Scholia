## Statement

The public errata record [#9525](https://openstax.org/errata/9525) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 4 section 4 https://cnx.org/contents/1Q9uMg_a@13.41:py6Hulz7@9/4-4-Uniform-Circular-Motion** and states the following (submitted 2019-09-09; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Throughout the section you call the components of the acceleration a_C and a_T, but in Figure 4.22 and Example 4.12, you refer to the tangential acceleration component as a_r. This is very confusing because it is inconsistent and it does not even refer to the radial component (which is a_C in your notation).  Please fix this.

Publisher's resolution:

> Figures 4.22 and 4.23 will be updated to use T instead of r.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
