## Statement

The public errata record [#20514](https://openstax.org/errata/20514) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Answer Key: Chapter 2** and states the following (submitted 2022-09-08; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The conversion from polar to Cartesian coordinates is correct, but the distance between P1 at (2.165m, 1.250m) and P2 at (-1.900, 3.290 m), by the Pythagorean Theorem as suggested, should be sqrt((-1.9-2.165)^2+(3.290-1.250^2), or 4.55 meters. The "back of the book" answer incorrectly says 5.27m.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "6.09" to "4.55".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
