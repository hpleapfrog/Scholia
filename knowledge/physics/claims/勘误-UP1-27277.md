## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#27277](https://openstax.org/errata/27277) 针对 **Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.7 Solving Problems in Physics** 记录如下
（提交 2025-05-24；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> Change
>
> =====
> This step is extremely important: –the goal of physics is to describe nature accurately.
> =====
>
> to
>
> =====
> This step is extremely important: the goal of physics is to describe nature accurately.
> =====
>
> as I believe the "–" to be unnecessary. As indicated by feeding Google the double-quoted string "a colon followed by a dash" and/or the double-quoted string "a colon followed by a hyphen", there is abundant reputable commentary to be found online pertaining to the typographical construction ":-", which according to Wikipedia possesses the formal name "colon dash compound point". Please be aware that due to historical reasons some of this commentary is a bit off colour.

出版方处理意见：

> Our reviewers accepted this change, and it will be included in the next print cycle.
