## Statement

The public errata record [#26665](https://openstax.org/errata/26665) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.5 Free Fall** and states the following (submitted 2025-02-18; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> For Example 3.14 the accompanying Figure 3.27 shows measurements at 1-s intervals for the position and velocity. Here the most important question is not asked: "extract the value of the downwards acceleration from the given measurements". Therefore I urge to modify the question accordingly. (Note that the current two questions do NOT require any calculation, because the answers are given as measurement data in the figure: it shows the ball hits the ground after 4 s with a speed of -44.1 m/s.)

Publisher's resolution:

> This figure will be updated.

> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
