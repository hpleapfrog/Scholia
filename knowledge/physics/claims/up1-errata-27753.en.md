## Statement

The public errata record [#27753](https://openstax.org/errata/27753) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Section 2.1 Scalars and Vectors** and states the following (submitted 2025-09-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> When vector A⃗is antiparallel to vector B⃗, we draw them along one line in either head-to-head fashion (Figure 2.7(c)) or tail-to-tail fashion. The magnitude of the vector difference, then, is the absolute value D=|A−B| of the difference of their magnitudes. The direction of the difference vector D⃗ is parallel to the direction of the longervector.""
>
> "Tell us in detail about the error and your suggestion. Regarding the instruction to "draw [the two antiparellel vectors]along one line in either head-to-head fashion (Figure 2.7(c)) ortail-to-tail fashion" I'd mention something akin to a one-dimensional "tail-to-head geometric construction" as a third option.……

Publisher's resolution:

> This figure and text will be updated.
