## Statement

The public errata record [#20547](https://openstax.org/errata/20547) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.2 Acceleration Vector** and states the following (submitted 2022-09-12; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> There was an earlier errata that addressed this same figure, but I believe the caption for the figure is still incorrect. As best as I can tell, the role of the red stars and blue dots are reversed. The blue dots are the projection onto the xy plane, and the red stars are the actual positions in 3-D.

Publisher's resolution:

> In the caption, revise "blue dots" to "red stars" and "red stars" to "blue dots".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
