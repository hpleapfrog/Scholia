## Statement

The public errata record [#20920](https://openstax.org/errata/20920) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Chapter Review** and states the following (submitted 2022-10-24; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> 1) The problem gives the speeds as 4 m/s and 7 m/s, but the solutions guide uses 4.0 and 7.0, which are different significant figures. 
>
> 2) The instructor solutions guide gives the calculation of -7 sin(60 degrees) + 4.0 = -2.0, which is rounded wrong. -7 sin(60 degrees) + 4.0 = -2.06217782649, which rounds to -2.1.
>
> 3) Furthermore, it then uses that rounded value in sqrt( (-2.0)^2 + (3.5)^2 ) instead of using the exact calculation, resulting in 4.03 instead of of 4.06.
>
> 4) The answer given is 4.03, which is again different significant figures from both the calculations used (4.0 and 7.0), and the given values (4 and 7)

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "4 m/s" to "4.0 m/s" and "7 m/s" to "7.0 m/s".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
