## Statement

The public errata record [#27315](https://openstax.org/errata/27315) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.4 Uniform and Nonuniform Circular Motion** and states the following (submitted 2025-06-07; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The vector is given correctly in the above solution as:
> 0.147iˆ−0.095jˆ m
>
> However, the solution given in the corresponding figure omits a '0' from the y-component:
> 0.147iˆ−0.95jˆ (m)

Publisher's resolution:

> This figure will be updated.
