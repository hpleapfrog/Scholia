## Statement

The public errata record [#29258](https://openstax.org/errata/29258) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Chapter Review: Additional Problems** and states the following (submitted 2026-04-17; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Question itself is stated ambiguously; it asks to "Compare the distance traveled of an object ... with an object ..." and never says whether these two objects had the same initial velocity. Suggest modifying "... over the same time period" to say "... over the same time period starting with the same initial velocity."
>
> *ASSUMING* the same initial velocity (which reasonable problem-solvers could assume), the given solution has mistakes in it. Solution says x1 = (3/2)*v0*t, and that's wrong.
