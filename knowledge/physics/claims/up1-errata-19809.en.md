## Statement

The public errata record [#19809](https://openstax.org/errata/19809) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions Section 4.2 Acceleration Vector** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-28; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Not t is approaching to nought but the change of t.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "t" to "Δt".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
