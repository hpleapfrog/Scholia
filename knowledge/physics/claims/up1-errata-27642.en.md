## Statement

The public errata record [#27642](https://openstax.org/errata/27642) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Section 2.2 Coordinate Systems and Components of a Vector** and states the following (submitted 2025-08-13; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The magnitude of vector D is computed incorrectly. The formula sqrt(4.0^2 + 2.9^2) is correct, but the result is shown as 4, and should be 4.9.

Publisher's resolution:

> This issue was addressed in another report and will be included in the next print cycle.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
