## Statement

The public errata record [#20637](https://openstax.org/errata/20637) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Section 5.6 Common Forces** and states the following (submitted 2022-09-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> These equations should not be boxed or numbered. They are not fundamental and are often incorrect (that is, they apply only to limited situations). Equation numbers in the rest of the book seem to be limited to fundamental statements (e.g. F = ma, w = mg). By applying equation numbers and highlighting with boxes, students may perceive these 4 equations as equally fundamental. This is especially problematic with the normal and tension forces, which are a source of wide-spread misconceptions, such as those given in these equations (e.g. N = mg).

Publisher's resolution:

> The formatting on these equations will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
