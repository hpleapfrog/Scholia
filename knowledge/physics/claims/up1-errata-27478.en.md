## Statement

The public errata record [#27478](https://openstax.org/errata/27478) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.2 Acceleration Vector** and states the following (submitted 2025-07-11; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The initial position of the skier (75, -50) is not on the slope of 15 degrees.
> The position should be (75, -20).

Publisher's resolution:

> This example will be updated in the next print cycle.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
