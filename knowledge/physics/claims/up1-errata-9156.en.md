## Statement

The public errata record [#9156](https://openstax.org/errata/9156) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 3, Section 3.2, Equation 3.7** and states the following (submitted 2019-08-06; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Equation 3.7 is totally wrong expression to highlight because it has no general applicability. The correctness of the expression depends on the specific form of x(t), given in the paragraph before, *and* its applicability is so narrow that even in Examples 3.3 and 3.4 (for which I think Equation 3.7 was given a number), it is not directly applicable. Equation 3.7 technically only applies when x(t) takes the form of a single-term polynomial, a situation so narrow that it almost never happens except in constant-velocity motion (both Examples 3.3 and 3.4 involve polynomials with multiple terms).……

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the sentence right before equation 3.7 to "If each term in the x(t) equation has the form of At^n where A is a constant and n is an integer, this can be differentiated using the power rule to be:" Then after the equation box, add the sentence "Note that if there are additional terms added together, this power rule of differentiation can be done multiple times and the solution is the sum of……
