## Statement

The public errata record [#5507](https://openstax.org/errata/5507) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 4 Example 4.8** and states the following (submitted 2018-02-15; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> For Chapter 4 Example 4.8, theta_v is calculated incorrectly on page 177. Theta_v should be written as tan^(-1)(-15.9/21.2)=36.9 degrees instead of 53.1 degrees.
>
> Do not forget to replace 53.1 with 36.9 in the Significance section as well.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise calculation of theta_v to tan^(–1)(–15.9/21.2) = 36.9 degrees.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
