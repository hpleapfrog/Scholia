## Statement

The public errata record [#27155](https://openstax.org/errata/27155) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.1 The Scope and Scale of Physics** and states the following (submitted 2025-05-07; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Change
>
> =====
> In other words, if numbers differ by one order of magnitude, the one is larger than the other by about ten.
> =====
>
> to
>
> =====
> In other words, if numbers differ by one order of magnitude, the one is larger than the other by a multiplicative factor of about ten.
> =====
>
> assuming, of course, that the degree to which "the one is larger than the other" is determined by subtraction. (Working through some mathematical analysis of the concept of order of magnitude in the course of formulating this suggested correction leads me to further suggest adding some language, or perhaps an exercise, considering the order of magnitude of the number 0.)

Publisher's resolution:

> The text will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
