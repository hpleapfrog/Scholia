## Statement

The public errata record [#9867](https://openstax.org/errata/9867) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 5, Section 6 (Common Forces)** and states the following (submitted 2019-10-07; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> In the section on normal forces, just before check your understanding 5.8, it has an outline of the trigonometry used to find the components of weight. In the text part, it says “Wy = w cos(theta) = mg sin(theta)” This sin part should be a cos. It has the correct version in Figure 5.23, but the text version is incorrect.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "sin" in the first line to "cos".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
