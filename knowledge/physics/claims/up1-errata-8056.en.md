## Statement

The public errata record [#8056](https://openstax.org/errata/8056) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 5 Example 5.16 "Coupled Blocks"** and states the following (submitted 2019-02-24; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The Free-Body diagram for mass m1 is not correct. The Normal Force N should point up and the gravitational force m1g should point down.
>
>  Also, the solution incorrectly states that the acceleration vectors a1 and a2 are equal, however they point in different directions. The magnitudes of the accelerations are equal, but not the vectors.

Publisher's resolution:

> This figure was updated in errata 6454. The text in the Significance section will be updated to "...assuming the string remains taut, the magnitudes of acceleration are equal. Thus, we have have │a →│_1 = │a→│_2 . If..."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
