## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#20393](https://openstax.org/errata/20393) 针对 **Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Section 2.2 Coordinate Systems and Components of a Vector** 记录如下
（提交 2022-08-31；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> The xyz axes in figure 2.21 are confusing since previously in the section the y axis points towards the top of the screen and the x axis points to the the right.
>
> In the current diagram the y axis points to the right of the screen and the x axis points out of the screen. The z axis points where the y axis used to point in all previous diagrams. This seems to add a needless layer of complication for students new to the topic.

出版方处理意见：

> Add the following to the end of the paragraph before Figure 2.21:
> Notice that while previously the y-axis was used for the upward direction, in three dimensions we usually choose z to represent the vertical. A right-handed coordinate system with z up and with one of the remaining axes pointing to the right and the other out of the page must have the y-axis pointing right and x out of the page, as……
