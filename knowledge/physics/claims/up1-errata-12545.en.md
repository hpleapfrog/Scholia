## Statement

The public errata record [#12545](https://openstax.org/errata/12545) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.5 Estimates and Fermi Calculations** and states the following (submitted 2020-07-12; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The sentence "Estimates also allow us perform 'sanity checks' on calculations..." is missing the word "to" before the word "perform."

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
