## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#28131](https://openstax.org/errata/28131) 针对 **Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.2 Acceleration Vector** 记录如下
（提交 2025-10-20；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> I have three things to say:
> 1- In chapter 4 nowhere in the book there is a written formula for average acceleration. I think for consistency it should be included.
> 2- In chapter 4 nowhere in the book acceleration vector is openly written as a(t)=a_x(t)i + a_y(t)j + a_z(t)k. I think for consistency it should be included.
> 3- On page 156 acceleration vector is written as a= a_0x i + a_0y j but later in equation 4.11-4.18 it is used as a_x and a_y. This is inconsistent and I think it should be a_x and a_y everywhere since we are not really interested in initial value of a -it is constant all the time.
> Best.

出版方处理意见：

> Our reviewers accepted suggestions 2 and 3, and the text will be updated in the next print cycle. 
>
> For suggestion 1, our reviewers determined this would require a significant book revision. While we cannot make this change at this time, we will consider it for future editions of this book.
