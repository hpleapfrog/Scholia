## Statement

The public errata record [#9848](https://openstax.org/errata/9848) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**In Chapter 5 (Newton’s Laws of Motion) on problem 36** and states the following (submitted 2019-10-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Customer Support submitting errata, Case # 39523
>
> Suggested Correction:
> I came across a typo in one of the problems. In Chapter 5 (Newton’s Laws of Motion) on problem 36, it says that an SUV is traveling at 1.00x10^3 km/h, which works out to be about 621 miles per hour. I checked all of the copies of this textbook, the hardcover print, the online pdf, and the iphone app and they all say the same number. The only one that is different is on the textbook answers page that is given to the instructors, the problem is repeated the same way but in the calculation of the answer, it uses 100 km/h.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "1.00 × 10^3" to "1.00 × 10^2".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
