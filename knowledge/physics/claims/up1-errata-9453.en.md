## Statement

The public errata record [#9453](https://openstax.org/errata/9453) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 4, figure 4.5** and states the following (submitted 2019-09-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The magnitude for the x-direction vector should be 4787 km not 4797 km. 4787 km is used in example 4.1.

Publisher's resolution:

> This figure will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
