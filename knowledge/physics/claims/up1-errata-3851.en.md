## Statement

The public errata record [#3851](https://openstax.org/errata/3851) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 3, Section 4** and states the following (submitted 2017-08-15; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The units are messed up in the next-to-last line of the Solution section in Example 3.9 (Calculating Final Velocity). It should be (m^2)/(s^2), as in the line that follows it.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
