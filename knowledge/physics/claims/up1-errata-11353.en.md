## Statement

The public errata record [#11353](https://openstax.org/errata/11353) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 2, Check your understanding 2.7** and states the following (submitted 2020-02-18; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Rounding error in magnitude calculation of vector. sqrt(15^2+31.7^2+2.5^2) = 31.159, which should be rounded to 31.2 whereas the answer key says 31.1.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "35.1" to "35.2" in the answer.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
