## Statement

The public errata record [#27772](https://openstax.org/errata/27772) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.2 Instantaneous Velocity and Speed** and states the following (submitted 2025-09-05; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Coefficient of 2nd term in x(t) has 1 significant figure (0.5 m/s^3). Considering coefficient of other term represented as (3.0 m/s), no reason to believe these are exact quantities. Final answer given as 9.5 m/s. So, either state or imply that coefficients of position are known as exact quantities (in which case, 3 m/s is sufficient for first term), or follow sig fig conventions.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "0.5" to "0.50".
