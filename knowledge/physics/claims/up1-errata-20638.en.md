## Statement

The public errata record [#20638](https://openstax.org/errata/20638) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Section 5.6 Common Forces** and states the following (submitted 2022-09-20; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The spring force equation should have a box and an equation number. It is a "fundamental" equation, equivalent to w = mg.

Publisher's resolution:

> This equation will be numbered and set in a box. It will also be added to the key equations list.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
