## Statement

The public errata record [#25992](https://openstax.org/errata/25992) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Answer Key: Chapter 2** and states the following (submitted 2024-10-24; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> 69.c)
> (A * F)(D x B)
> A & F are antiparallel, A*F = |A| |F| cos(180) = 10*20* -1 = -200
> DxB = (-15.97, 12.04) x ( 3.01, 3.99)
> (-15.97*3.99 - 12.04*3.01) = -100k
> -100k * -200 = 20,000k
> I don't think it should be +-

Publisher's resolution:

> The answer will be updated.
