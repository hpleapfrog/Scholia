## Statement

The public errata record [#17641](https://openstax.org/errata/17641) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.5 Estimates and Fermi Calculations** and states the following (submitted 2021-09-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The sentence "You develop these skills by thinking more quantitatively and by being willing to take risks." needs grammatical revision. - GM

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the sentence to " "You develop these skills by thinking quantitatively and being willing to take risks."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
