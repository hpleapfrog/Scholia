## Statement

The public errata record [#19665](https://openstax.org/errata/19665) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.5 Relative Motion in One and Two Dimensions** and states the following (submitted 2022-05-10; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> It would be prudent to mention that almost all the equations in this section are approximations that break down near the speed of light.

Publisher's resolution:

> Add the following to the end of the paragraph before the Relative Motion in One Dimension heading:
> This method makes an approximation that breaks down near the speed of light, where the more accurate methods or special relativity are needed, but is extremely accurate for everyday speeds (Relativity).

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
