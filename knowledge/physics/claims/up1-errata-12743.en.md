## Statement

The public errata record [#12743](https://openstax.org/errata/12743) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 4 Motion in Two and Three Dimensions: Section 4.4 Uniform Circular Motion** and states the following (submitted 2020-08-08; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The tangential acceleration vector is mislabeled "a_r" instead of "a_T" (two locations)

Publisher's resolution:

> This issue was addressed in another report and is correct in webview.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
