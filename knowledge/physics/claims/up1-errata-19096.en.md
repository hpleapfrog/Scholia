## Statement

The public errata record [#19096](https://openstax.org/errata/19096) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Chapter Review: Problems** and states the following (submitted 2022-03-02; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The attached picture is extremely unclear. It isn't at all clear that pulley 4 is in contact with the foot. It also seems odd that the length of the force vectors are different, but their lengths have no physical significance. That is very confusing.

Publisher's resolution:

> The figure will be updated. Also add to the end of the question: "Express answers in terms of the unknown masses and angle."

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
