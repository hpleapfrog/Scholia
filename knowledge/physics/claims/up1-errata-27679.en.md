## Statement

The public errata record [#27679](https://openstax.org/errata/27679) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 1 Units and Measurement: Section 1.6 Significant Figures** and states the following (submitted 2025-08-26; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The text states, "In some topics, particularly in optics, more accurate numbers are needed and we use more than three significant figures." I believe this should say that more "precise" numbers are needed.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "accurate" to "precise".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
