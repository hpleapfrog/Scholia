## Statement

The public errata record [#5131](https://openstax.org/errata/5131) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 2 Problems section** and states the following (submitted 2018-02-01; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The figure for Problem 28 (page 96) shows vectors with magnitudes and angles indicated. The relative lengths of those vectors are drawn correctly. For example, lengths of vector A and vector F looked almost same while their magnitudes are 10 and 20 units. The the correctness of the angles should be checked as well.

Publisher's resolution:

> Replace with updated figure.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
