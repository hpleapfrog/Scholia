## Statement

The public errata record [#27308](https://openstax.org/errata/27308) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Section 2.4 Products of Vectors** and states the following (submitted 2025-06-05; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Change
>
> =====
> For vector A⃗ = A_{x}iˆ + A_{y}jˆ + A_{z}kˆ in a rectangular coordinate system, use Equation 2.29 through Equation 2.32 to show that A⃗ ⋅iˆ = A_{x} A⃗ ⋅jˆ = A_{y} and A⃗ ⋅kˆ = A_{z}.
> =====
>
> to
>
> =====
> For vector A⃗ = A_{x}iˆ + A_{y}jˆ + A_{z}kˆ in a rectangular coordinate system, use Equation 2.29 through Equation 2.32 to show that A⃗ ⋅iˆ = A_{x}, A⃗ ⋅jˆ = A_{y}, and A⃗ ⋅kˆ = A_{z}.
> =====
>
> , adding commas for clarity.

Publisher's resolution:

> The text will be updated to include commas.
