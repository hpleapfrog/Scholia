## 陈述

OpenStax《University Physics Volume 1》的公开勘误记录 [#6893](https://openstax.org/errata/6893) 针对 **Chapter 4, Problem 59** 记录如下
（提交 2018-09-12；状态 Completed；处理 Approved）：

> As a student pointed out, the position of the quarterback is not given, and the football fans among our students will know that the quarterback is unlikely to throw the ball from the same "yard line" that the wide receiver started from. One solution would be "Aaron Rodgers throws the football at 20 m/s to his wide receiver, who is running straight down the field at 9.4 m/s. If Aaron throws the football when the receiver is 10.0 m in front of him, what angle does Aaron have to launch the ball at so the receiver catches it 20.0 m in front of Aaron?"

出版方处理意见：

> Revise the question to "Aaron Rogers throws a football at 20.0 m/s to his wide receiver, who is running straight down the field at 9.4 m/s. If Aaron throws the football when the wide receiver is 10.0 m in front of him, what angle does Aaron have to launch the ball at so the receiver catches it 20.0 m in front of Aaron?"
