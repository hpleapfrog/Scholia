## Statement

The public errata record [#19594](https://openstax.org/errata/19594) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Chapter Review** and states the following (submitted 2022-04-27; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The instructor's manual solution is correct, but the final answer is not correct. The incorrect final answer is also listed in the textbook solution (student facing).
>
> 45. A particle moves in a straight line with an initial velocity of 30 m/s and constant acceleration 30 m/s2. (a) What is its displacement at t = 5 s? (b) What is its velocity at this same time?
> Solution
> a. answer given is 375 m - should be 525 m
> b. answer given is 150 m/s - should be 180 m/s

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the answer to part a to "525 m" and the answer to part b to "v = 180 m/s".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
