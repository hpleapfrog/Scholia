## Statement

The public errata record [#6223](https://openstax.org/errata/6223) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 5, #79, page 261** and states the following (submitted 2018-05-30; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> I believe there is an error in the answer to problem 79, an “additional problem” of chapter 5. I believe the error arises from flipping, or exchanging out the x and y components of the F3 vector when finding your net force, which leads to an erroneous acceleration answer. 
> service ticket #23312

Publisher's resolution:

> This solution will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
