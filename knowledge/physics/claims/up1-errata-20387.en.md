## Statement

The public errata record [#20387](https://openstax.org/errata/20387) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Section 2.2 Coordinate Systems and Components of a Vector** and states the following (submitted 2022-08-30; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> "Even in everyday life we naturally invoke the concept of orthogonal projections in a rectangular coordinate system. For example, if you ask someone for directions to a particular location, you will more likely be told to go 40 km east and 30 km north than 50 km in the direction 37° north of east."
>
> This is apparently the only definition of orthogonal projection presented in the text.
>
> Clearly the author knows what it is but a new reader might not.
>
> ". In the Cartesian system, the x and y vector components of a vector are the orthogonal projections of this vector onto the x- and y-axes, respectively.……

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "orthogonal projections" to "vector components" in the first paragraph. Revise "In the Cartesian
> system, the x and y vector components of a vector are the orthogonal projections..." to "In the Cartesian
> system, the x and y vector components of a vector are the orthogonal projections, as illustrated in Figure 2.16,...".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
