## Statement

The public errata record [#1984](https://openstax.org/errata/1984) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 2: Vectors, Section: Products of Vectors** and states the following (submitted 2016-10-22; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> It turns out that there is no universally agreed upon definition of the term "lever arm". It would be nice if the authors of this textbook would settle on a definition and use it consistently. Unfortunately, that did not happen.
>
> In the chapter on vectors the lever arm is defined as the vector from the axle to the point of application of the force. Note: this version of the lever arm is a vector. In the chapter in which torques are defined, the lever arm is re-defined as the perpendicular distance from the axle to the line of action of the force.……

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
