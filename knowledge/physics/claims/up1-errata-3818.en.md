## Statement

The public errata record [#3818](https://openstax.org/errata/3818) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 3, Section 2** and states the following (submitted 2017-08-11; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> There are two mistakes in the Strategy section of Example 3.3 (titled "Instantaneous Velocity Versus Average Velocity"). 1) At the beginning, "give" should be "gives." 2) Toward the end, it should be Equation 3.3 to calculate the average velocity, not Equation 3.6.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our reviewers accepted this change.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
