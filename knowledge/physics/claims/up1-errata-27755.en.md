## Statement

The public errata record [#27755](https://openstax.org/errata/27755) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 3 Motion Along a Straight Line: Section 3.2 Instantaneous Velocity and Speed** and states the following (submitted 2025-09-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> I suggest changing=====("... we find the expression for the instantaneous velocity:", "... =dx(t)/dt.")
> =====
> To
> =====
> ("... we find the expression for the instantaneous velocity:", "... =dx(t)/dt = d/dt x(t).")
> =====
> or adding a brief mention of "dx(t)/dt = d/dt x(t)" just prior to the "Instantaneous Velocity" inset containing "Equation 3.4", which uses"... = d/dt x(t)".

Publisher's resolution:

> The text will be updated.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
