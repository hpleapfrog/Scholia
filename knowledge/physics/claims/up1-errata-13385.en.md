## Statement

The public errata record [#13385](https://openstax.org/errata/13385) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1. Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Chapter Review: Problems** and states the following (submitted 2020-09-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> This problem has already been revised once. The new version, visible online Fall 2020, reads:
>
> "A team of nine members on a tall building tug on a string attached to a large boulder on an icy surface. The boulder has a mass of 200 kg and is tugged with a force of 2350 N. (a) What is magnitude of the acceleration? (b) What force would be required to produce a constant velocity?" 
>
> The suggested solution to this problem, indicated in Errata revision 9847, is: "Revise the answer to: a. 1.95 m/s^2 b. 1960 N"
>
> It is unclear why nine members (of what group?) are involved in this problem, and what the height of the building is, which is presumably some height above an icy surface.……

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise the question to: 
> "A team of nine members each engage in a tug-of-war, pulling in opposite directions on a horizontal rope. Each of the first team’s members has an average mass of 68 kg and exerts an average force of 1350 N horizontally on the ground as they pull on the rope.……

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
