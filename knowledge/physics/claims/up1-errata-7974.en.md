## Statement

The public errata record [#7974](https://openstax.org/errata/7974) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Chapter 4, Example 4.1, Figure 4.4, page 160, version 12.5** and states the following (submitted 2019-02-14; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Although the overall calculation would be correct, the x-component for vector r(t2) should be expressed in terms of angle -45 deg and not +45 deg, i.e. cos(-45), to match the expression for the y-component and Figure 4.4.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "(cos 45°)" to "(cos(−45°))".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
