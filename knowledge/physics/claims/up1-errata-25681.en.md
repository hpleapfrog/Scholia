## Statement

The public errata record [#25681](https://openstax.org/errata/25681) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Answer Key: Chapter 4** and states the following (submitted 2024-09-04; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The answer to 29e shows r(t) (which isn't really part of the answer), then a large vertical gap of blank space, then the actual answer of v average. The large gap caused many of my students to think that r(t) was the answer and they completely missed the v(t) shown further down. If you don't look carefully, it looks like v(t) is part of the answer to next problem. Removing r(t) and the gap would make the answer much more readable.
>
> This gap shows up in the online version and PDF versions.

Publisher's resolution:

> Our team is working to resolve this issue.

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
