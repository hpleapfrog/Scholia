## Statement

The public errata record [#22816](https://openstax.org/errata/22816) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 2 Vectors: Chapter Review: Problems** and states the following (submitted 2023-09-05; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> Problem 61. Insufficient information given to correctly and accurately answer the problem. Only positional-altitude and angles (azimuth and above xy-plane) of velocity vector are given. Exact bearing, distance, and speed relative to the tower are all missing (the information given for each aircraft is just an altitude and 3D orientation of velocity vector with no information on actual speed or where on the xy-plane the aircraft may be located).……

Publisher's resolution:

> This problem will be updated.
