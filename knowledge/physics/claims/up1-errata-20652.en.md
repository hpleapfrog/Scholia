## Statement

The public errata record [#20652](https://openstax.org/errata/20652) for OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* concerns
**Unit 1 Mechanics: Chapter 5 Newton's Laws of Motion: Section 5.4 Mass and Weight** and states the following (submitted 2022-09-21; status Completed;
resolution Approved):

> The book currently states, "Weight varies dramatically if we leave Earth’s surface. On the Moon, for example, acceleration due to gravity is only 1.67 m/s^2. A 1.0-kg mass thus has a weight of 9.8 N on Earth and only about 1.7 N on the Moon."
>
> However, this value of 1.67 m/s^2 is not accurate. On the lunar surface, g is 1.625 m/s^2 (1.63 m/s^2 to 2 sig figs). Therefore, the 1.0-km mass would have a weight of 1.6 N on the Moon rather than 1.7 N. 
>
> Please fix the value of g_Moon and the weight of the mass on the Moon.

Publisher's resolution:

> Revise "1.67" to "1.62" and "1.7 N" to "1.6 N".

> [!NOTE]
> This entry claims only that "the publisher recorded and accepted this erratum".
> **It does not claim that this repository checked the current web text**; cite the
> permanent link when verifying.
