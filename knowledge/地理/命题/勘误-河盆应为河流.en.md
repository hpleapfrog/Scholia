## Statement

p.56, question 2a originally reads:

> Find the **河盆** order of the river network in area Z.

It should be:

> Find the **stream** order of the river network in area Z.

## Nature

**Terminological misuse**, and **not a typo but a concept conflation**:

- **河盆** (drainage basin) is the collecting area — a **spatial extent**
- **河流等级** (stream order) is the hierarchical division of a drainage network — a **structural property**

"河盆 order" is not a valid concept in the discipline. This kind of error is more dangerous than a typo:
a student following the question would **glue the two concepts together**, and the confusion is hard to notice.

> [!WARNING]
> This is a **"wording trap"**, not a mere typo, and belongs on the explicit correction list when teaching.
