## Statement

《高中活學地理（第三版）》 (reprinted with corrections 2024) has contour-label errors on **several topographic maps**.
The OUP official errata list at least **6**, all of the kind **figure does not match the text**:

| # | Location | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | p.19 fig 15 | "the 60 m contour" | delete |
| 2 | p.22 fig 18 | "the 200 m contour" | delete |
| 3 | p.24 fig 20 | the 50 m and 100 m contours | modify |
| 4 | p.26 fig 22a | "the 140 m contour" | delete |
| 5 | p.26 fig 23a | "the 100 m contour" | delete |
| 6 | p.40 fig 37 | the 250 m (left) and 50 m (middle) contours; and modify the arrows "B" "F" "H" | delete / modify |

## Argument

### A1 · Publisher's statement (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: the publisher states at least 6 topographic-map labels in the textbook are wrong
- **Premise**: the OUP official errata list the 6 items above one by one (ev-014)
- **Reasoning**: the errata are published by the publisher and give page and figure numbers item by item, constituting an explicit statement
- **Evidence**: ev-014
- **Attacked**: none
- **Attribution**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-05

### A2 · Systematic inference (undecided, to check)

- **Claim**: the 6 errors come from **a defect in the same batch of the mapping process**, so other topographic maps in the volume may be wrong too
- **Premise**: the 6 errors concentrate in the single type "contour labels" and are spread across p.19–p.40 in different chapters
- **Reasoning**: if they were isolated typos, the types would be scattered; clustering of one type points to a **process cause**
- **Evidence**: (none) **no direct evidence yet**
- **Attacked**: may be coincidence, or the errata only collected reader-reported items — **unreported does not mean no error**
- **Attribution**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-05
- **Status**: **undecided**, the other topographic maps need checking

> [!WARNING]
> **A2 is inference, not conclusion.** It is recorded to mark a **direction to check** —
> do not read it as an established fact.

## Why merged into one

The 6 errors could have been split into 6 propositions, but **merged they carry more information**:
seen singly, each is a delete/modify instruction; seen together, **they point to a possible systematic defect** —
and that is the judgment the user actually needs.

> [!NOTE]
> This is a **modeling choice**: record "multiple instances of the same underlying cause" as one entry.
> If later checking shows the causes differ, split it.
