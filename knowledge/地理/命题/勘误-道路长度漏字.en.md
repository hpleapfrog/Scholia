## Statement

p.13, question "怎樣量度兩地的實際直線距離？", point 1 originally reads:

> Measure the **度** of the road segment from P to Q with a ruler.

**One character is missing**; it should be:

> Measure the **length** of the road segment from P to Q with a ruler.

## Argument

### A1 · Publisher's statement (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: the publisher states a character is missing here, corrected to "道路的長度" (the road's length)
- **Premise**: the OUP official errata, "Geography skills" column, p.13 entry (ev-014), listing both the original and the correction
- **Reasoning**: the publisher gives both the "original" and the "corrected" columns, which is an explicit statement about the defect at this spot
- **Evidence**: ev-014
- **Attacked**: none
- **Attribution**: @hpleapfrog · 2026-10-05

## Nature

**A typo (omission)** — the lightest class of defect, but with real consequence:
"量度道路的**度**" is not a word in Chinese, so a self-studying student gets stuck right on the teaching point.
