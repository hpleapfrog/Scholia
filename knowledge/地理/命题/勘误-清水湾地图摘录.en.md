## Statement

p.45, paragraph 2, line 1 originally reads:

> See also the **清水灣** 1:20,000 map extract……

It should be:

> See also the 1:20,000 map extract……

i.e. **delete** the "清水灣" place qualifier.

## Argument

### A1 · Publisher's statement (no attack yet)

- **Claim**: the publisher states "清水灣" should be deleted here
- **Premise**: the OUP official errata list the original and the corrected content (ev-014)
- **Reasoning**: the publisher gives both columns, making the misreference explicit
- **Evidence**: ev-014
- **Attacked**: none

## Nature

**A cross-reference error**: it sends the reader to a map **not attached to this book**.
The consequence is the student cannot find the corresponding material — **in a self-study setting this breaks the flow outright**.
