## Observations, sorted by severity

Three kinds of problems in this errata are worth separating:

| Type | Entry | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| **概念混用** concept conflation | 河盆 / 河流 | a student following it **glues the two concepts together**, and won't notice |
| **系统性可疑** possibly systematic | 等高线 6 处 | the same kind of error clusters, pointing to a mapping-process problem |
| **会过期** will age | cross-section | changes with editorial guidance, needs re-review |
| 一般 general | 漏字、指涉 | self-evident |

**The teaching consequences of these four are entirely different, so they must not be lumped into one "error rate".**
